## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- Explain why written communication carries particular risk, given its permanence

## Core Content

This curriculum's PURPLE track covered discrimination law's specific protected categories and the legal risk created by misrepresentation. Here's the "why" a leader's everyday communication style connects directly to both: comments made in performance reviews, emails, or casual conversation can become evidence in a discrimination or wrongful termination claim if they touch on a protected category, even if the leader didn't intend any discriminatory meaning — and public or written promises about job security, compensation, or advancement can create the same kind of misrepresentation exposure covered elsewhere in this curriculum's discussion of marketing and sales claims.

Here's the "why" written communication carries particular risk: unlike a verbal comment that might be disputed based on memory, a written email, text message, or performance review creates a permanent, easily verifiable record — exactly the kind of evidence a legal dispute depends on, meaning careless writing carries more durable risk than an equally careless verbal comment made in the moment.

Before sending a significant written communication — a performance review, a difficult email, a public statement — would this specific wording hold up if it were read aloud in a legal proceeding months or years from now?

## Worked Example

[ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] A leader writes an informal, casual email to a colleague commenting on an employee's performance in a way that touches on a personal characteristic unrelated to actual job performance. [ASSUMPTION] If that employee is later terminated for a legitimate performance reason and brings a discrimination claim, this earlier email — never intended as anything but casual commentary — becomes durable, written evidence a court might consider relevant to the claim, illustrating why a leader's everyday writing carries legal weight regardless of its casual, off-the-cuff intent.
