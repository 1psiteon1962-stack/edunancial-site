## Core Content

This curriculum's foundational teaching device distinguishes self-employment (where a person's own direct labor and presence generate the income) from genuine business ownership (where systems and staff generate income independent of any single person's constant presence). Here's the "why" this distinction sets a hard growth ceiling for a presence-dependent operation: if every unit of output requires the owner's own direct involvement — a specific decision only they can make, a relationship only they can manage, a task only they know how to do — the business's total capacity is limited by that single person's available hours, no matter how hard they work or how much demand exists.

Here's the "why" systems remove this ceiling: a documented process, a trained team member who can make a decision without escalating it, a repeatable way of managing a relationship — these transfer capability that was previously locked inside one person's head and calendar into something the organization can execute without that person's direct, constant involvement, directly connecting to the delegation-as-risk-management principle from Lesson 2.

What specific tasks or decisions in your business currently require your direct, personal involvement that couldn't be handled by a documented system or a trained team member? What would it take to change that?

## Worked Example

[ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] A business owner personally handles every significant client relationship, every pricing decision, and every quality check, meaning the business's total output is capped by how many hours this one person can personally work. [ASSUMPTION] By documenting the decision criteria used for pricing, training a team member to manage client relationships using a clear playbook, and establishing a quality check system that doesn't require the owner's personal review of every item, the business's capacity is no longer limited to what one person can personally do — echoing directly the systems-enable-scale principle at the heart of this curriculum's foundational teaching device.
