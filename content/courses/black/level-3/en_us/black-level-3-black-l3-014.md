## Core Content

This curriculum's PURPLE track covered the specific legal minimums governing employment relationships — at-will employment limits, discrimination protections, wage and hour rules. Here's the "why" genuine employer responsibility extends beyond simply satisfying these legal minimums: legal compliance establishes a floor, not a ceiling, for how employees should be treated — a business can be entirely legally compliant while still treating employees in ways that fail to reflect genuine respect for their dignity and contribution.

This matters with particular weight in many economies globally, where a single job doesn't just support one individual but an entire extended family depending on that income — hiring staff, in these contexts, carries a responsibility that extends well beyond the immediate employment relationship itself, directly connecting to a broader theme in this curriculum: business ownership and leadership carry a genuine responsibility to the people whose livelihoods depend on the business's decisions, a responsibility that exists independent of, and beyond, whatever the specific legal minimums happen to require.

Beyond what's legally required, what does your organization actually do to reflect genuine respect for your employees' dignity and the broader impact your decisions have on the people and families who depend on this employment?
