## Core Content

Lesson 18 established that a formal corporate board exists to provide oversight and accountability on behalf of the organization and its stakeholders. Here's the "why" a leader also benefits from a genuinely distinct, informal **personal board of advisors** — a small group of trusted individuals a leader consults for perspective on decisions, including ones a formal corporate board might never see: a corporate board's fiduciary duty runs to the organization, not to the individual leader personally, meaning it isn't structured or positioned to provide the kind of personal, candid perspective on a leader's own individual growth, wellbeing, or career decisions that a personal advisory network can.

This directly extends the themes from Lessons 23 and 37: just as outside perspective strengthens a negotiation and an ongoing network functions as risk mitigation, a personal board of advisors provides a leader with genuinely independent, personally invested perspective — distinct from, and a valuable complement to, the organizationally-focused oversight a formal corporate board provides.

Who currently serves this role for you personally — people you'd genuinely turn to for candid perspective on your own growth and decisions, separate from your organization's formal oversight structure? If the honest answer is no one, what would it take to build that?

## Worked Example

[ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] A leader has a well-functioning formal corporate board providing genuine organizational oversight, exactly as Lesson 18 described.
