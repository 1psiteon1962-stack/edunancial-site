## Main Lesson

### 1. Registering a Business Name

Formally registering your business name with the relevant government authority. [VERIFIED FACT] This step is what typically allows a business to open a bank account and be legally recognized apart from the owner's personal name.

### 2. Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Equivalent

A tax identification number issued to a business by the relevant tax authority. [REGION-SPECIFIC RULE] Requirements and equivalent numbers vary by country; some regions use a different identifier for tax and business registration purposes.

### 3. Local and Industry Licenses

Permits required to legally operate, which vary by location and industry. [REGION-SPECIFIC RULE] A food business, for example, often requires health permits that a consulting business would not need.

### 4. Staying Compliant Over Time

Registration is not a one-time event — many jurisdictions require renewals or annual filings. [ASSUMPTION] Assume renewal and reporting obligations exist unless you've confirmed otherwise for your specific region.
