## Main Lesson

### 1. Cost-Plus Pricing

Setting a price by adding a fixed markup to the cost of producing a good or service. [VERIFIED FACT] This is simple to calculate but does not account for what customers are actually willing to pay.

### 2. Value-Based Pricing

Setting a price based on the perceived value to the customer rather than production cost alone. [VERIFIED FACT] This approach often yields higher margins but requires a clear understanding of customer priorities.

### 3. Competitive Pricing

Setting a price relative to what competitors charge for similar offerings. [VERIFIED FACT] This can be a reasonable starting point but risks a race to the bottom if used as the only strategy.

### 4. Testing and Adjusting Price

Prices are rarely perfect on the first attempt and often need adjustment based on real sales data. [OPINION] Many businesses treat their initial price as a hypothesis to test rather than a permanent decision.


## Key Definitions
