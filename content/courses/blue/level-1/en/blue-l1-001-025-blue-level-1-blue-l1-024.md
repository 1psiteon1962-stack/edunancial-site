## Main Lesson

### 1. Systems Before Headcount

Documenting how tasks are done before hiring people to do them. [OPINION] Many operators find that hiring into an undocumented process just multiplies confusion rather than adding capacity.

### 2. Delegation vs. Abdication

Delegation means handing off a task with clear expectations and follow-up; abdication means letting go without oversight. [VERIFIED FACT] Confusing the two is a common reason delegated tasks fail early on.

### 3. Building a Simple Org Structure

Defining who owns which area of the business as it grows. [ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] Even a two-person team benefits from a clear split, such as one person owning operations and the other owning sales.

### 4. Founder Bottlenecks

Points where a business's growth is limited by the founder's personal time or decision-making. [OPINION] Many growth advisors treat founder bottlenecks as the most common ceiling small businesses hit before their first real scale-up.
