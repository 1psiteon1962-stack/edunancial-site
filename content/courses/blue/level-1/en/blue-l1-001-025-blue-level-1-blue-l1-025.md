## Main Lesson

### 1. Reviewing the Building Blocks

This capstone draws on structure, costs, pricing, cash flow, marketing, and hiring covered in Lessons 011-024. [VERIFIED FACT] Treat this lesson as an integration exercise rather than new material.

### 2. Assembling a One-Page Plan

Condensing the business plan into a single page covering problem, customer, revenue model, and next milestones. [ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] A one-page format forces clarity and is easier to revisit and update than a lengthy document.

### 3. Identifying Your Riskiest Assumption

The single assumption that, if wrong, would most threaten the business's viability. [OPINION] Many experienced founders test their riskiest assumption first, before investing further time or money.

### 4. Setting a 90-Day Action Plan

Breaking the plan into concrete actions for the next 90 days. [ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] This might include finalizing pricing, registering the business, and securing the first three customers.
