# BLUE-L1-013: Understanding Customers and Customer Needs

## Learning Objectives
1. Define a customer in business terms.
2. Distinguish a want from a need in the context of a purchase.
3. Explain why understanding the customer is the starting point of any business.

## Core Content
*VERIFIED FACT:* A customer is the person or organisation that pays for a business's product or service. Every business exists to solve a problem or fulfil a want for someone, and that someone's willingness to pay is what makes the business viable.

*ASSUMPTION:* A "need" is generally something a customer requires to solve a real problem, while a "want" is generally something that improves their situation without being essential — many successful businesses are built on wants rather than strict needs, so this distinction shapes marketing more than it determines viability.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A plumber solves a need — a burst pipe must be fixed. A specialty coffee shop sells a want — customers could make coffee at home, but they're willing to pay for convenience, quality, and experience. Both are entirely legitimate businesses; understanding which one you're offering shapes how you price, market, and compete.

## Practice Quiz
1. What is a customer, in business terms?
2. What generally distinguishes a want from a need?
3. True/False: Only businesses that meet a strict need can be viable.
4. Why does understanding the customer matter as a starting point?

## Answer Key
1. The person or organisation that pays for a product or service   2. A need solves a real problem; a want improves a situation without being essential   3. False   4. The customer's willingness to pay is what makes a business viable

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the basic definition of a customer.
2. This is the general distinction between wants and needs.
3. Many viable businesses are built around wants, not just strict needs.
4. Without a clear picture of the customer, a business has no basis for its offering.

## Key Takeaways
- A customer is whoever pays for the product or service a business offers.
- Wants and needs are both legitimate bases for a viable business.
- Understanding the customer is the starting point for pricing, marketing, and competing.
