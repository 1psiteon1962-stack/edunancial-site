# BLUE-L1-017: Cost-Plus Pricing

## Learning Objectives
1. Define cost-plus pricing.
2. Calculate a simple cost-plus price.
3. Identify a limitation of relying solely on cost-plus pricing.

## Core Content
Cost-plus pricing means calculating the cost to produce a product or deliver a service, then adding a fixed margin on top to set the selling price. *VERIFIED FACT:* Price = Cost + (Cost × Desired Margin Percentage).

*ASSUMPTION:* A commonly cited limitation of cost-plus pricing is that it ignores what customers are actually willing to pay — a business could underprice a highly valued offering, or overprice one that customers don't value as much as the cost suggests, since the method starts entirely from internal costs rather than external demand.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A furniture maker calculates that a chair costs $80 in materials and labour to produce. Wanting a 50% margin, they price it at $80 + ($80 × 0.50) = $120. This method gives a clear, defensible price, but it doesn't account for whether customers would happily pay $150 for the same chair, or whether $120 is already too high for that market.

## Practice Quiz
1. What is cost-plus pricing?
2. What is the formula for cost-plus pricing?
3. If a product costs $50 and the desired margin is 40%, what is the price?
4. What does cost-plus pricing commonly fail to account for?

## Answer Key
1. Calculating cost, then adding a fixed margin to set price   2. Price = Cost + (Cost × Desired Margin Percentage)   3. $70   4. What customers are actually willing to pay

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the fundamental definition of cost-plus pricing.
2. This is the standard cost-plus pricing formula.
3. $50 + ($50 × 0.40) = $50 + $20 = $70.
4. Cost-plus pricing starts from internal costs, not external customer demand.

## Key Takeaways
- Cost-plus pricing sets price by adding a fixed margin to production cost.
- The formula is straightforward: Cost + (Cost × Margin Percentage).
- It commonly overlooks what customers are actually willing to pay.
