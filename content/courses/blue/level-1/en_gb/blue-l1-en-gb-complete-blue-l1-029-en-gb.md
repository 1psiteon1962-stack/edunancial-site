# BLUE-L1-029: Hiring Basics

## Learning Objectives
1. Identify the general stages of a hiring process.
2. Explain why a clear job description matters before hiring.
3. Recognise common costs involved in hiring beyond salary.

## Core Content
*ASSUMPTION:* A hiring process generally involves several stages: defining the role, advertising it, screening candidates, interviewing, and making an offer — skipping the first step (a clear job description) commonly leads to hiring mismatches later.

*VERIFIED FACT:* The cost of hiring an employee generally extends well beyond their salary, commonly including recruitment costs, training time, equipment, and employer-side taxes or contributions, which vary by jurisdiction.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A small business owner hires quickly to fill an urgent gap, without clearly defining what the role actually needs. Three months later, the new hire's skills don't match what the business actually required, and the owner has to restart the process — having spent time and money without solving the original problem. A clear job description at the start, defining the specific tasks and skills needed, would have helped avoid this mismatch.

## Practice Quiz
1. Name the general stages of a hiring process.
2. Why does a clear job description matter before hiring?
3. True/False: The cost of hiring an employee is limited to their salary.
4. Name a commonly cited cost of hiring beyond salary.

## Answer Key
1. Defining the role, advertising, screening, interviewing, offering   2. It helps avoid hiring mismatches later   3. False   4. Recruitment costs, training time, equipment, or employer-side taxes (among others)

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the general sequence of a hiring process.
2. A clear job description sets expectations that guide the rest of the process.
3. Hiring costs generally extend beyond salary alone.
4. These are commonly cited additional costs of hiring.

## Key Takeaways
- Hiring generally involves defining, advertising, screening, interviewing, and offering.
- A clear job description at the outset helps prevent later hiring mismatches.
- The true cost of hiring extends well beyond salary alone.
