# BLUE-L1-032: What Is Entrepreneurship?

## Learning Objectives
1. Define entrepreneurship.
2. Distinguish entrepreneurship from simply owning any business.
3. Identify commonly cited traits associated with entrepreneurship.

## Core Content
Entrepreneurship is the process of identifying an opportunity, taking on risk, and organising resources to build something new — typically a business — around that opportunity. *OPINION:* Entrepreneurship is commonly associated with traits like tolerance for uncertainty, willingness to take calculated risks, and persistence, though these are generalisations rather than requirements, and successful entrepreneurs vary widely in temperament.

*ASSUMPTION:* Not every business owner is necessarily acting as an entrepreneur in the fullest sense — someone who buys a well-established franchise with a proven system takes on some risk, but the degree of uncertainty and innovation involved differs from someone building an entirely new business from an original idea.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* Two people each start a business. One opens a franchise location of an established coffee chain, following a proven operating manual. The other develops an original product from scratch, with no existing market to point to. Both are business owners, but the second is commonly seen as engaging more fully in entrepreneurship, given the higher uncertainty and originality involved.

## Practice Quiz
1. What is entrepreneurship?
2. Name a commonly cited trait associated with entrepreneurship.
3. True/False: Every business owner is automatically acting as an entrepreneur in the fullest sense.
4. What distinguishes the two business owners in the worked example?

## Answer Key
1. Identifying an opportunity, taking on risk, and organising resources to build something new   2. Tolerance for uncertainty, willingness to take calculated risks, or persistence   3. False   4. The degree of uncertainty and originality involved in what they built

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the fundamental definition of entrepreneurship.
2. These are commonly cited, though generalised, traits.
3. The degree of entrepreneurial risk and originality varies by situation.
4. This illustrates the general distinction between following a proven system and building something original.

## Key Takeaways
- Entrepreneurship involves identifying opportunity, taking on risk, and organising resources to build something new.
- Commonly cited traits include tolerance for uncertainty and persistence, though these are generalisations.
- Not every business owner engages in entrepreneurship to the same degree.
