# BLUE-L1-033: Identifying a Business Opportunity

## Learning Objectives
1. Define a business opportunity.
2. Identify common sources of business opportunities.
3. Explain why solving a real problem matters more than a clever idea alone.

## Core Content
A business opportunity is a gap between what customers need or want and what's currently available to meet that need. *ASSUMPTION:* Common sources of opportunity include a personal frustration with an existing product or service, a change in technology or regulation opening new possibilities, or simply noticing that an underserved group of customers exists.

*OPINION:* Many experienced business builders commonly emphasise that solving a real, validated problem matters more than the cleverness of an idea alone — an original idea that doesn't address something customers will actually pay to solve isn't yet a viable business opportunity.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* An entrepreneur notices that a specific type of small business in their area struggles to find affordable bookkeeping help, because most providers focus on larger clients. That gap — real businesses with a real unmet need — is a business opportunity, distinct from an idea for a product nobody has actually asked for.

## Practice Quiz

## Answer Key
1. A gap between what customers need or want and what's currently available   2. Personal frustration, changes in technology/regulation, or an underserved customer group   3. False   4. Solving a real, validated problem

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the fundamental definition of a business opportunity.
2. These are commonly cited sources of opportunity.
3. Cleverness alone doesn't guarantee customers will pay for a solution.
4. This reflects commonly cited advice from experienced business builders.

## Key Takeaways
- A business opportunity is a gap between customer needs and current offerings.
- Common sources include personal frustration, changing conditions, and underserved groups.
- Solving a real, validated problem matters more than cleverness alone.
