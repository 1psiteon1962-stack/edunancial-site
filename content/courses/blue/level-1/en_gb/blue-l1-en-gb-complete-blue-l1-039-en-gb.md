# BLUE-L1-039: Business Insurance Basics

## Learning Objectives
1. Define business insurance in general terms.
2. Identify commonly cited types of business insurance.
3. Explain the general purpose insurance serves for a business.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific types, requirements, and names of business insurance vary by country; this lesson describes general concepts only.

Business insurance protects a business against specific financial losses from events like property damage, liability claims, or employee injury. *ASSUMPTION:* Commonly cited types include general liability insurance (covering claims of injury or damage caused by the business), property insurance (covering physical assets), and professional liability insurance (covering claims of errors or negligence in professional services provided).

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A customer slips and is injured in a shop. Without liability insurance, the business might have to pay any resulting claim entirely out of pocket, potentially threatening its finances. With appropriate liability insurance in place, the insurer generally covers the claim up to the policy's limits, in exchange for the premiums the business has been paying.
