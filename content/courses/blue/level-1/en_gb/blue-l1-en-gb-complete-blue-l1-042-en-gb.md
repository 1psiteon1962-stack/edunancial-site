# BLUE-L1-042: Sales Basics

## Learning Objectives
1. Define sales in business terms.
2. Identify the general stages of a sales process.
3. Explain why listening matters more than persuading in effective selling.

## Core Content
Sales is the process of converting a potential customer's interest into an actual purchase. *ASSUMPTION:* A sales process generally involves several stages: identifying a prospective customer, understanding their needs, presenting a relevant solution, addressing concerns or objections, and closing the sale.

*OPINION:* Many experienced salespeople commonly emphasise that listening to understand a customer's actual needs matters more than simply persuading — a salesperson who understands the real problem can present a genuinely relevant solution, rather than pushing a generic pitch that doesn't fit.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A salesperson who launches straight into a rehearsed pitch without asking questions might describe features the customer doesn't care about. A salesperson who first asks what the customer actually needs, then tailors their presentation to address that specific need, is generally far more likely to close the sale and leave the customer satisfied.
