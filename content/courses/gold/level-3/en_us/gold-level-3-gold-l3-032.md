## Core Content

This track has covered diversification across asset classes (Lesson 14) and across individual holdings within an asset class (a concept from this curriculum's WHITE track). Here's a third, related but distinct dimension: diversifying **across time** — spreading investment decisions across different points in time, rather than concentrating them all at a single moment.

Here's the "why" this is a genuinely separate form of diversification: even a well-diversified portfolio across asset classes and individual holdings is still exposed to a single moment's specific market conditions if the entire portfolio is established all at once. Dollar-cost averaging (Lesson 19) is the most direct application of this principle — spreading purchases across many different points in time specifically addresses the risk of any single entry point being poorly timed, a risk that diversifying across assets or holdings alone doesn't fully address.

[VERIFIED FACT] This is why a genuinely comprehensive risk-management approach often considers all three dimensions together — which assets to hold, how concentrated or diversified those holdings are, and over what timeline decisions to establish and adjust those positions — rather than treating diversification as addressed once asset and holding-level diversification alone have been achieved.

## Worked Example

[ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] An investor builds a well-diversified portfolio across multiple asset classes and many individual holdings within each — addressing two dimensions of diversification. If she establishes this entire portfolio in a single lump-sum transaction, however, her whole portfolio is still exposed to that single moment's specific market conditions across the board. [ASSUMPTION] Spreading that same portfolio's establishment across multiple purchases over time, rather than all at once, adds the third dimension of time diversification, reducing her exposure to the specific risk that her single chosen entry point happened to be poorly timed across her entire portfolio simultaneously.
