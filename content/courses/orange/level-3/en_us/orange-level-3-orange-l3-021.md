## Core Content

This curriculum's PURPLE track established that regulatory compliance is a distinct legal obligation category, enforced directly by government agencies. Here's the "why" this matters with particular specificity for advertising: [VERIFIED FACT] certain industries — financial services, healthcare, alcohol, and others — generally face additional, specific advertising regulations beyond general consumer protection rules, given the particular risks associated with misleading claims in those specific contexts. Similarly, different advertising mediums (broadcast, print, digital, direct mail) can be subject to different specific rules and disclosure requirements.

This is why a marketing approach that's entirely compliant in one industry or medium might not be compliant in another — a claim or format acceptable in a general consumer product advertisement might face additional specific disclosure requirements or restrictions if used to advertise a regulated industry's product or service, and a business operating in, or advertising to, multiple industries or through multiple mediums needs to understand that compliance isn't a single, uniform standard.

Before launching a new marketing campaign, especially one entering a new medium or touching a specific regulated topic, what industry-specific or medium-specific rules might apply that a general advertising compliance review wouldn't catch?

## Worked Example

[ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] A business operating in a general consumer products industry expands into offering a financial or healthcare-adjacent product, applying the same general marketing approach and claims style it has always used. [ASSUMPTION] This same marketing approach, while previously compliant for its original product line, may fail to meet the additional, specific advertising regulations applicable to the new, regulated industry — illustrating why entering a new industry or medium requires a fresh, specific compliance review, not an assumption that general marketing practices automatically transfer.
