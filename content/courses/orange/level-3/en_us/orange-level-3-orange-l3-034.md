## Core Content

Modern marketing relies heavily on customer data — tracking behavior, building targeted advertising audiences, personalizing outreach. Here's the "why" this practice is subject to real legal regulation, connecting directly to the regulatory compliance concept covered in this curriculum's PURPLE track: [VERIFIED FACT] many jurisdictions have enacted specific data privacy regulations governing how customer data can be collected, used, shared, and stored, often including specific consent requirements, disclosure obligations, and rights for individuals to access or request deletion of their own data — rules that apply directly to common marketing practices like tracking, retargeting, and email list building.

This is why marketing strategy and data privacy compliance need to be planned together, not treated as separate concerns handled by entirely different teams without coordination: a marketing tactic that's technically effective can simultaneously violate specific data privacy requirements, creating the same kind of regulatory enforcement risk covered generally in the PURPLE track, entirely independent of whether the marketing itself achieves its intended business goal.

Before implementing a new data-driven marketing tactic — a new tracking method, a new way of building targeted audiences — has it been reviewed against the specific data privacy requirements applicable in the jurisdictions where your customers are located?

## Worked Example

[ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE] A marketing team implements a new customer tracking and targeting method that proves highly effective at improving conversion rates, without specifically reviewing it against applicable data privacy regulations. [ASSUMPTION] The method turns out to violate specific consent or disclosure requirements in some of the jurisdictions where the business's customers are located, creating regulatory compliance risk connecting directly to the PURPLE track's enforcement discussion — a risk entirely separate from, and unaffected by, the tactic's genuine marketing effectiveness.
