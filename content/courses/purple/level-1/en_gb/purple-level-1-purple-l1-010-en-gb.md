# PURPLE-L1-010: Basic Consumer Rights

## Learning Objectives
1. Recognise the general purpose of consumer protection laws.
2. Name common categories of consumer rights.
3. Recognise that specific protections vary by legal system.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific consumer protection laws vary significantly by country and region; this lesson describes only general concepts.

*ASSUMPTION:* Consumer protection laws generally aim to balance the relationship between businesses and consumers, since businesses often have more information and bargaining power. Common categories of consumer rights include protection against misleading advertising, the right to safe products, and protections relating to contracts and warranties.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A business that advertises a product as "defect-free" while knowing there is a known failure rate could be violating laws against misleading advertising in many legal systems. These laws generally exist because the individual consumer has no practical way to verify such claims before buying, so the law steps in to discourage deception.
