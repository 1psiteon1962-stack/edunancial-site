# PURPLE-L1-017: What Is a Will?

## Learning Objectives
1. Define will in simple terms.
2. Recognise the general purpose of a will.
3. Recognise common elements a will generally includes.

## Core Content
*VERIFIED FACT:* A will is a legal document that sets out how a person wants their estate distributed after death, and can also name a guardian for minor children and an executor responsible for administering the estate.

*ASSUMPTION:* The general purpose of a will is to give a person control over what happens to their property and affairs after death, rather than leaving these decisions entirely subject to the general default rules that would apply without a will.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A person might draft a will specifying that their house go to a particular child, that certain personal items go to specific friends, and that a trusted sibling act as executor to manage the process. Without a will, these specific decisions generally could not be made according to the person's exact wishes, and general default rules would apply instead.

## Practical Quiz
1. What is a will?
2. What is the general purpose of a will?
3. True/False: A will can only deal with physical property.
4. What role might a will name besides beneficiaries?

## Answer Key
1. A legal document that sets out how a person's estate is distributed after death   2. Giving control over what happens to personal property and affairs   3. False   4. An executor or a guardian for minor children

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the fundamental definition of a will.
2. This is the commonly cited general purpose of a will.
3. A will can also name guardians and executors, not just distribute property.
4. Wills commonly name executors and, where applicable, guardians for minor children.

## Key Points to Remember
- A will is a legal document that sets out how a person's estate is distributed after death.
- Its general purpose is to give control over personal and estate decisions.
- It can also name an executor and, where applicable, a guardian for minor children.
