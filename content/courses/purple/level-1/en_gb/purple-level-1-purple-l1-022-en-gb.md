# PURPLE-L1-022: Small Claims Court

## Learning Objectives
1. Define small claims court in simple terms.
2. Recognise the general purpose of this court.
3. Recognise that financial limits vary by legal system.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific financial limits and procedures for small claims court vary by country and region; this lesson describes only the general concept.

Small claims court is designed to resolve disputes involving relatively small amounts of money, generally faster, simpler, and cheaper than an ordinary civil court. *ASSUMPTION:* These courts often allow people to bring and defend their own cases without needing a lawyer, with simplified procedures designed to be accessible to people without legal training.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* Someone overcharged by a contractor for minor work might bring a case in small claims court rather than hiring a lawyer and filing a full civil action, since the amount in dispute would generally be too small to justify the cost and complexity of ordinary civil litigation.
