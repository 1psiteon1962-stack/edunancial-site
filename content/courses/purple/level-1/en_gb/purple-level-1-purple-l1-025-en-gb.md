# PURPLE-L1-025: Hiring a Lawyer — The Basics

## Learning Objectives
1. Recognise general factors to consider when hiring a lawyer.
2. Explain why a lawyer's specialism is generally important.
3. Recognise the importance of clarifying costs in advance.

## Core Content
*ASSUMPTION:* When hiring a lawyer, it is generally important to consider their area of specialism, since law is a broad field and a lawyer specialising in one area (such as property law) is generally not the best choice for a matter in a completely different area (such as criminal law).

*VERIFIED FACT:* Clarifying the fee structure in advance is a commonly recommended practice before hiring a lawyer, since legal fees can vary significantly depending on the structure (hourly, fixed fee, or contingency fee) and the matter can turn out more costly than expected without a clear understanding from the outset.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* Someone facing eviction should probably seek a lawyer specialising in landlord-tenant law rather than a generalist business lawyer, since the specialist will generally have deeper knowledge of the specific applicable laws and procedures. Before hiring them, clearly asking how fees will be charged — hourly, with a fixed fee, or another structure — generally helps avoid unpleasant surprises later.
