# PURPLE-L1-026: Lawyer Fees — Common Structures

## Learning Objectives
1. Recognise common lawyer fee structures.
2. Explain what a "contingency fee" arrangement is.
3. Recognise a typical situation where each structure is used.

## Core Content
*VERIFIED FACT:* Common lawyer fee structures include the hourly rate (the client pays for each hour of the lawyer's work), the fixed fee (a one-off agreed amount for a specific service), and the contingency fee (the lawyer is only paid if they win the case, generally receiving a percentage of the compensation obtained).

*ASSUMPTION:* Contingency fee arrangements are commonly used in personal injury cases, since they allow someone without immediate financial resources to pay fees upfront to still access legal representation, with the lawyer taking on the risk of receiving nothing if the case is unsuccessful.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* Someone needing a simple will drawn up might pay an agreed fixed fee upfront, since the scope of the work is predictable. Someone injured in a car accident with no money available to pay legal fees upfront, on the other hand, might hire a lawyer under a contingency fee arrangement, with the lawyer receiving a percentage only if compensation is obtained.
