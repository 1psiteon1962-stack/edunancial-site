# PURPLE-L1-027: Attorney-Client Privilege

## Learning Objectives
1. Define attorney-client privilege in simple terms.
2. Explain the general purpose of this protection.
3. Recognise that this protection generally has certain exceptions.

## Core Content
*VERIFIED FACT:* Attorney-client privilege is a legal protection that generally prevents a lawyer from disclosing private communications with their client without the client's consent, and generally prevents those communications from being forced into disclosure in court.

*ASSUMPTION:* The general purpose of this protection is to encourage clients to be completely honest with their lawyers, since a client who fears shared information could be used against them would probably hide important facts, making it harder for the lawyer to provide effective representation.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A client who tells their lawyer embarrassing details about what actually happened in a case can generally trust that this information will remain private, which allows the lawyer to prepare the best possible strategy with the full picture. Without this protection, many clients would probably leave out crucial information out of fear, leaving the lawyer working with an incomplete picture.
