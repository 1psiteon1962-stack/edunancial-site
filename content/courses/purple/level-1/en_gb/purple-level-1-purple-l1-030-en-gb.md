# PURPLE-L1-030: Employment Law — The Basics

## Learning Objectives
1. Define employment law in general terms.
2. Recognise common areas covered by employment law.
3. Recognise that specific protections vary by legal system.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific employment laws vary significantly by country and region; this lesson describes only general areas.

Employment law is the body of law governing the relationship between employers and workers. *VERIFIED FACT:* Common areas covered by employment law generally include minimum wage, working hours, workplace safety, discrimination, and rights relating to termination of employment.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A worker working more hours than permitted without the additional compensation required by law might have a claim under employment laws relating to working hours. Similarly, a worker injured due to unsafe working conditions might have protections under workplace safety laws — both situations illustrate how employment law generally seeks to protect workers in the employment relationship.
