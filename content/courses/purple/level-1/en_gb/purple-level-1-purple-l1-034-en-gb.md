# PURPLE-L1-034: Landlord-Tenant Law

## Learning Objectives
1. Recognise common general obligations of landlords.
2. Recognise common general obligations of tenants.
3. Recognise that landlord-tenant laws vary by legal system.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific landlord-tenant laws vary significantly by country and region; this lesson describes only general concepts.

*VERIFIED FACT:* Landlords generally have an obligation to keep the property in habitable condition — which often includes working plumbing, adequate heating, and safe structures — while tenants generally have an obligation to pay rent on time and avoid damaging the property beyond ordinary wear and tear.

*ASSUMPTION:* Many disputes between tenants and landlords generally arise from unclear expectations about repairs, deposits, or the exact terms of the tenancy agreement — a clear written agreement generally helps prevent misunderstandings about each party's responsibilities.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* If a flat's heating stops working in winter, the landlord generally has an obligation to repair it within a reasonable time, since working heating is generally considered part of keeping the property habitable. If instead the tenant caused significant damage to the walls beyond ordinary wear and tear, the tenant would generally be responsible for that damage at the end of the tenancy.

- Landlords generally must keep the property in habitable condition.
- Tenants generally must pay rent on time and avoid damage beyond ordinary wear and tear.
- A clear written tenancy agreement generally helps prevent disputes.
