# PURPLE-L1-036: Family Law — The Basics

## Learning Objectives
1. Define family law in general terms.
2. Recognise common areas covered by family law.
3. Recognise that family laws vary significantly by legal system.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific family laws vary enormously by country and region; this lesson describes only general areas.

Family law is the area of law dealing with family relationships and domestic matters. *VERIFIED FACT:* Common areas covered by family law generally include marriage, divorce, child custody, child support, and adoption.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A couple deciding to separate legally would generally need to address several family law matters at once — how to divide shared property, whether spousal support applies, and, where children are involved, how custody and child support will be shared. Each of these matters is generally governed by specific rules within that legal system's family law.
