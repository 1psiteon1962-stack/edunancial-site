# PURPLE-L1-042: Bail Explained

## Learning Objectives
1. Define bail in general terms.
2. Explain the general purpose of the bail system.
3. Recognise that practices vary by legal system.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific bail practices and rules vary significantly by country and region; this lesson describes only the general concept.

Bail is a sum of money or other security that a person accused of an offence pays to be released while awaiting trial, on the understanding that the money is forfeited if the person fails to attend required hearings. *ASSUMPTION:* The general purpose of this system is to balance two interests: allowing an accused (but not yet convicted) person to keep their liberty while the case is pending, while giving them a financial incentive to attend the required court proceedings.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* Someone arrested and accused of an offence might have a hearing where a judge sets the bail amount, considering factors such as the seriousness of the charge and the risk that the person won't attend trial. If the person pays the set bail, they can generally be released while awaiting trial; if they fail to attend required hearings, they generally forfeit that money and could face additional charges.
