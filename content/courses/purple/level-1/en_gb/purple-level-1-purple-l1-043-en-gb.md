# PURPLE-L1-043: Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

## Learning Objectives
1. Define a non-disclosure agreement in general terms.
2. Distinguish a one-way NDA from a mutual NDA.
3. Explain a common situation where an NDA is used.

## Core Content
*VERIFIED FACT:* A non-disclosure agreement (commonly called an "NDA") is a contract in which one or more parties agree to keep certain information confidential and not disclose it to third parties without authorisation.

*ASSUMPTION:* A **one-way** NDA generally involves only one party sharing confidential information, with the confidentiality obligation resting solely on the other party, while a **mutual** NDA generally involves both parties sharing confidential information with each other, with both bound to secrecy.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A company hiring an outside consultant to review its confidential business plans would generally ask them to sign a one-way NDA, since only the company is sharing sensitive information. Two companies exploring a possible merger and needing to share confidential financial information with each other, on the other hand, would generally sign a mutual NDA, since both parties are revealing sensitive information.

## Practical Quiz
1. What is a non-disclosure agreement (NDA)?
2. What is a one-way NDA?
3. True/False: In a mutual NDA, only one party has the confidentiality obligation.
4. In the merger example, why would a mutual NDA be used?

## Answer Key
1. A contract in which the parties agree to keep certain information confidential   2. Only one party shares information, and only the other party has the confidentiality obligation   3. False   4. Because both companies are sharing sensitive information with each other

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the fundamental definition of a non-disclosure agreement.
2. This is the fundamental definition of a one-way NDA.
3. In a mutual NDA, both parties generally have the confidentiality obligation.
4. Both parties sharing sensitive information is the general reason to use a mutual NDA.

## Key Points to Remember
- An NDA is a contract in which the parties agree to keep certain information confidential.
- A one-way NDA means only one party shares information; a mutual NDA means both do.
- The choice between the two generally depends on who is sharing sensitive information in the relationship.
