# PURPLE-L1-047: Data Protection and Privacy — The Basics

## Learning Objectives
1. Define data protection in general terms.
2. Recognise common categories of personal data often protected by law.
3. Recognise that privacy laws vary significantly by legal system.

## Core Content
*REGION-SPECIFIC RULE:* Specific data protection and privacy laws vary significantly by country and region; this lesson describes only general concepts.

Data protection refers to laws and practices designed to safeguard people's personal information against misuse, unauthorised access, or inappropriate disclosure. *VERIFIED FACT:* Commonly protected categories include personal identification data (such as names and addresses), financial information, and health information, though the exact scope of protection varies by specific law.

## Practical Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A company collecting its customers' contact information would generally have obligations under applicable data protection laws regarding how it stores that information, who it can share it with, and what it must do if that data is compromised in a security breach. These obligations generally exist because individuals have a legitimate interest in controlling how their personal information is used.
