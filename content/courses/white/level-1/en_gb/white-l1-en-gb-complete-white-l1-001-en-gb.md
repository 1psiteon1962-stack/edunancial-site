# WHITE-L1-001: What Are Paper Assets?

## Learning Objectives
1. Define paper assets in plain terms.
2. Distinguish paper assets from real estate and physical assets.
3. Identify common categories of paper assets.

## Core Content
Paper assets are financial instruments representing ownership or a claim to value — shares, bonds, mutual funds, and similar securities — rather than physical property like real estate or gold. *VERIFIED FACT:* The term "paper" is historical, referring to when ownership was recorded on physical certificates; today these are almost entirely digital records.

Common categories include equities (shares), fixed income (bonds), and pooled investments (mutual funds, ETFs) — each representing a different way to gain financial exposure without owning a physical asset directly.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* Priya buys 10 shares of a company through her brokerage app. She owns a small fractional claim on that company's value and future profits — no physical object changes hands, no deed, no certificate posted to her. Her ownership exists entirely as an electronic record at her brokerage.

## Practice Quiz
1. What are paper assets?
2. Why is the term "paper" historical?
3. Name two common categories of paper assets.
4. True/False: Buying shares today typically involves a physical certificate.

## Answer Key
1. Financial instruments representing ownership or a claim to value   2. Ownership was historically recorded on physical certificates   3. Equities, fixed income (among others)   4. False

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the core definition distinguishing paper assets from physical property.
2. The name reflects an earlier era of paper-based record keeping.
3. Shares and bonds are foundational paper asset categories.
4. Modern ownership is almost entirely digital, not physical certificates.

## Key Takeaways
- Paper assets represent ownership or claims to value, not physical property.
- The term is historical — modern holdings are digital records.
- Shares, bonds, and pooled investments are core paper asset categories.
