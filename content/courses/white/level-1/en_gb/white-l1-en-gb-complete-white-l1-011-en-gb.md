# WHITE-L1-011: Diversification — Not All Eggs in One Basket

## Learning Objectives
1. Define diversification.
2. Explain how diversification can reduce risk.
3. Recognize that diversification doesn't eliminate all risk.

## Core Content
Diversification means spreading investments across different assets, sectors, or geographies so that poor performance in one area doesn't devastate the entire portfolio. *VERIFIED FACT:* Diversification can reduce the impact of any single investment's poor performance, but it does not eliminate all risk — broad market declines can still affect a well-diversified portfolio.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* Sam invests his entire savings in a single company's share. When that company has a bad year and drops 40%, his entire portfolio drops 40%. His friend Ravi spreads investments across 200 different companies via an index fund; when a few of those companies have bad years, the overall impact on Ravi's portfolio is far smaller, since strong performers elsewhere can offset weak ones.

## Practice Quiz
1. What is diversification?
2. True/False: Diversification eliminates all investment risk.
3. What happened to Sam's portfolio when his single share dropped 40%?
4. Why was Ravi's portfolio less affected by a few underperforming companies?

## Answer Key
1. Spreading investments across different assets to reduce concentrated risk   2. False   3. It also dropped 40%   4. Strong performers elsewhere offset the weak ones

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the core definition of diversification.
2. Diversification reduces, but doesn't eliminate, risk — broad declines still matter.
3. With all money in one share, its full decline hit his entire portfolio.
4. Spreading risk across many holdings cushions the impact of any single poor performer.

## Key Takeaways
- Diversification spreads risk across multiple investments.
- It reduces (but doesn't eliminate) the impact of any single investment's poor performance.
- Broad market-wide declines can still affect even well-diversified portfolios.
