# WHITE-L1-023: Reading a Share Quote

## Learning Objectives
1. Identify common elements shown in a share quote.
2. Distinguish current price from daily change.
3. Explain what trading volume indicates.

## Core Content
A share quote typically displays: the current price, the day's change (in dollars and percentage), trading volume (shares traded that day), and often the day's high/low range. *VERIFIED FACT:* Volume reflects how many shares changed hands, which can indicate how much investor interest or activity a share is experiencing on a given day.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A quote reads: Price $145.20, Change +$2.10 (+1.47%), Volume 3.2M, Day Range $142.80-$146.00. This tells an investor the share is currently trading at $145.20, up $2.10 (1.47%) from the previous close, with 3.2 million shares traded that day, fluctuating between $142.80 and $146.00 during the session.

## Practice Quiz
1. What does the "change" figure in a share quote show?
2. What does trading volume indicate?
3. True/False: The day's high/low range shows the share's all-time price range.
4. If a share's change shows +2%, what does that mean?

## Answer Key
1. The price movement from the previous close   2. How many shares traded that day / activity level   3. False   4. The price is up 2% from the previous close

## Answers and Explanations
1. This is the standard "change" metric in a share quote.
2. Volume reflects trading activity, not price direction itself.
3. The day range shows only that trading session's range, not all-time.
4. A positive percentage change indicates the price rose relative to the prior close.

## Key Takeaways
- A share quote shows price, change, volume, and day range at minimum.
- Volume reflects trading activity, distinct from price movement.
- Day range reflects that session only, not the share's historical range.
