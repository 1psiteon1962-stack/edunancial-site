# WHITE-L1-050: Common Investing Mistakes for Beginners

## Learning Objectives
1. Identify frequently cited beginner investing mistakes.
2. Explain why lack of diversification is a common early error.
3. Recognize the value of starting with foundational knowledge before complex strategies.

## Core Content
*OPINION:* Commonly cited beginner investing mistakes include: investing without an emergency fund first, insufficient diversification (concentrating too heavily in one share or sector), trying to time the market, ignoring fees, and jumping into complex strategies (like options) before understanding fundamentals.

## Worked Example
*ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE:* A new investor puts most of their savings into a single trending share without an emergency fund in place. When the share declines and they simultaneously face an unexpected expense, they're forced to sell investments at a loss to cover the emergency — a situation that a modest emergency fund, established before investing heavily, could have prevented.

## Practice Quiz
1. Name two commonly cited beginner investing mistakes.
2. Why is having an emergency fund relevant to investing decisions?
3. True/False: Jumping into complex strategies like options is commonly recommended for beginners.
4. What happened in the worked example due to lack of an emergency fund?

## Answer Key
1. Insufficient diversification, trying to time the market (among others)   2. It can prevent being forced to sell investments at a loss during unexpected expenses   3. False   4. The investor had to sell investments at a loss to cover an emergency expense

## Answers and Explanations
1. These are among the most commonly cited beginner mistakes.
2. An emergency fund provides a buffer separate from invested funds.
3. Building foundational knowledge is commonly recommended before complex strategies.
4. This illustrates the real consequence of skipping the emergency fund step.

## Key Takeaways
- Common beginner mistakes include poor diversification, market timing attempts, and skipping an emergency fund.
- An emergency fund helps avoid being forced to sell investments at a loss.
- Build foundational knowledge before pursuing complex strategies like options.
