# Sales Organization Leadership: Orchestrating Targets, Incentives, Ethics, and Customer Value

## Scenario Opener
ASSUMPTION/OPINION (hypothetical scenario for teaching purposes only): A software company sets aggressive quarterly sales targets. Revenue rises, but many new customers cancel within months because they were sold features they did not need.

## Learning Objectives
- Explain how sales targets and incentives shape behavior.
- Explain why customer value is central to sustainable sales.
- Identify the role of training and ethics in sales leadership.
- Apply an orchestrated approach to building a sales organization.

## Core Content
PRINCIPLE: Sales leadership is not only about closing deals. It is about creating customers who succeed and stay.

Targets should be challenging but realistic and linked to profitable, long-term revenue.

Incentives should reward revenue quality, such as customer retention and margin, not only new sales.

Training helps salespeople understand customers' problems and match solutions appropriately (ORANGE).

Ethics protects trust. Misleading claims or pressure tactics may produce short-term revenue but damage reputation and increase cancellations.

Sales leaders should coordinate with product, customer support, and finance to ensure promises can be delivered.

## Worked Example
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE: The software company adds customer retention to sales bonuses, introduces consultative sales training, and requires a needs assessment before larger contracts. Sales growth slows briefly, but cancellations fall sharply and long-term revenue rises.

C) It encourages sales that create lasting value
D) It prevents new sales

**Q3. Why is ethics important in sales?**

C) Customers prefer misleading claims
D) Ethics only applies to lawyers

A) To ensure promises can be delivered
B) Other departments are unrelated
C) Coordination slows all growth
D) Sales should operate alone

**Q5. What reduced cancellations in the example?**
A) Higher pressure tactics
B) Removing training
C) Increasing targets only
D) Retention-based incentives and consultative training

## Answer Key
Q1: D
Q2: C
Q3: B
Q4: A
Q5: D

## Answers and Explanations
**Q1.** Long-term customers create sustainable growth.

**Q2.** Incentives shape behavior.

**Q3.** Ethical selling builds lasting relationships.

**Q4.** Delivery depends on the whole organization.

**Q5.** Better alignment improved customer fit.

## Key Takeaways
- Sales success means customers who succeed and stay.
- Reward revenue quality, not just volume.
- Train for consultative, ethical selling.
- Coordinate sales promises with delivery capabilities.
