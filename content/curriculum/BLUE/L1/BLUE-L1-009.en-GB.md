# What Is a Partnership, and What Are the Real Risks of an Informal One?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define a general partnership in plain terms.
- Explain why an informal partnership is risky.
- Explain what a written partnership agreement can prevent.

## Core Content

### What a General Partnership Is

A general partnership forms when two or more people go into business
together, sharing profits, losses, and management — often without even
formally deciding to, if they're simply operating a business together
with no other structure in place.

### Ask Yourself This

If two friends start selling a product together with a handshake and
no written agreement, are they legally partners? In many places, yes —
by default, simply operating a business together can create a legal
partnership, whether or not anyone intended that.

### The Real Risk of "We'll Figure It Out"

In a general partnership, each partner is generally personally liable
not just for their own actions, but for the actions of the other
partner(s) taken on behalf of the business. Without a written agreement
covering things like ownership split, decision-making, and what happens
if someone wants out, disagreements can become expensive, personal
disputes with no clear resolution path.

## Worked Example

Two friends start a business together informally. One signs a supplier
contract without the other's knowledge, and the business can't pay it.
Ask yourself: is the partner who didn't sign it still on the hook?
Under general partnership law, quite possibly yes — each partner can be
personally liable for obligations the other partner created on the
business's behalf.

## Practice Quiz

1. A general partnership can form:
   A. Only with formal, filed paperwork
   B. Simply by two or more people operating a business together, even
      without formal paperwork
   C. Only between family members
   D. Only for licensed professions

2. True or False: In a general partnership, a partner is only liable for
   their own individual actions.

3. What can a written partnership agreement help prevent?
   A. All business risk of any kind
   B. Costly disputes over ownership, decisions, and exit terms
   C. The need to ever communicate with a partner
   D. Business taxes entirely

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. A general partnership can form by default, without
formal paperwork.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Partners can generally be liable for each
other's actions taken on the business's behalf.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. A written agreement helps prevent costly, unclear
disputes.

## Key Takeaways

- A general partnership can form by default, even without paperwork.
- Partners can generally be personally liable for each other's business
  actions.
- A written partnership agreement helps prevent costly, ambiguous
  disputes.
