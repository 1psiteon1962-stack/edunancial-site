# What Is a Break-Even Point?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define break-even point in plain terms.
- Explain, conceptually, what has to happen for a business to reach it.
- Explain why knowing this number matters before opening.

## Core Content

### What Break-Even Point Means

The break-even point is the level of sales at which a business's total
revenue exactly equals its total costs — no profit, no loss, the exact
tipping point between the two.

### What Has to Happen to Reach It

Ask yourself: below that point, is the business gaining or losing money?
Below break-even, expenses exceed revenue — a loss. Above it, revenue
exceeds expenses — a profit. This connects directly back to overhead
(the fixed costs that must be covered regardless of sales volume) from
two lessons ago.

### Why Knowing This Number Matters Before Opening

A business owner who knows their break-even point going in has a
concrete, specific target — not just a vague hope that "sales will be
good enough." Without that number, it's easy to underestimate how much
needs to be sold just to stop losing money, let alone start making a
profit.

## Worked Example

A small shop has $3,000/month in overhead and makes about $15 in profit
per item sold above its direct costs. Ask yourself: roughly how many
items does it need to sell each month just to break even? $3,000 ÷ $15
= 200 items — a concrete number the owner can measure actual sales
against, rather than just hoping things work out.

## Practice Quiz

1. The break-even point is:
   A. The point where a business makes maximum profit
   B. The sales level where total revenue equals total costs
   C. The business's total overhead cost
   D. A type of business license

2. True or False: Below break-even, a business is generating a profit.

3. Why does knowing the break-even point matter before opening a
   business?
   A. It doesn't matter at all
   B. It gives a concrete sales target instead of a vague hope
   C. It guarantees the business will succeed
   D. It eliminates the need for a business plan

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Break-even is where revenue exactly equals total
costs.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Below break-even, expenses exceed revenue — a
loss.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. It provides a specific, measurable target rather than
a vague hope.

## Key Takeaways

- Break-even is the sales level where revenue exactly equals total
  costs.
- Below it is a loss; above it is a profit.
- Knowing this number gives a concrete target before opening, not just
  hope.
