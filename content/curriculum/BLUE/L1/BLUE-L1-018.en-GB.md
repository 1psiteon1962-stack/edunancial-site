# What Is Bootstrapping?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define bootstrapping in plain terms.
- Explain the core trade-off of bootstrapping.
- Recognize it as a deliberate strategy, not just a default.

## Core Content

### What Bootstrapping Means

Bootstrapping means building a business using only its own revenue and
the owner's personal resources, deliberately avoiding outside investment
or significant debt.

### The Core Trade-Off

Ask yourself: what does an owner give up by taking outside investment,
and what do they give up by not taking it? Outside investment often
means faster growth but giving up some ownership and control.
Bootstrapping means keeping full ownership and control, but often
growing more slowly, limited by the business's own available cash.

### A Deliberate Strategy, Not Just "No Other Option"

While some businesses bootstrap because outside funding isn't
available, many owners choose it deliberately — valuing full control and
ownership enough to accept slower growth as the trade-off, not because
they had no other choice.

## Worked Example

An entrepreneur turns down an interested investor's offer, choosing
instead to grow using only the business's own profits. Ask yourself: is
this a mistake? Not necessarily — if retaining full control and
ownership matters more to that entrepreneur than the potential for
faster growth, bootstrapping is a rational, deliberate choice, not a
missed opportunity.

## Practice Quiz

1. Bootstrapping means:
   A. Raising money exclusively from venture capital
   B. Building a business using only its own revenue and the owner's
      resources
   C. Taking on significant business debt
   D. Selling the business to a competitor

2. True or False: Bootstrapping is always a sign that a business
   couldn't secure outside investment.

3. What is the core trade-off of bootstrapping compared to taking outside
   investment?
   A. No trade-off exists
   B. Full ownership and control, often at the cost of slower growth
   C. Guaranteed faster growth
   D. Bootstrapping eliminates all business risk

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Bootstrapping means relying on the business's own
resources.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Many owners bootstrap deliberately, by choice.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Full control and ownership, often traded for slower
growth.

## Key Takeaways

- Bootstrapping means growing a business using only its own resources.
- The core trade-off is full ownership/control versus often slower
  growth.
- It's frequently a deliberate strategic choice, not just a lack of
  options.
