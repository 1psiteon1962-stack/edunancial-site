# What Is an Invoice, and Why Do Payment Terms Matter So Much?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define an invoice in plain terms.
- Define payment terms and give an example.
- Connect payment terms directly to the cash flow lesson.

## Core Content

### What an Invoice Is

An invoice is a formal request for payment, sent by a business to a
customer, detailing what was provided and how much is owed.

### What Payment Terms Are

Payment terms specify when payment is actually due — commonly expressed
as something like "Net 30," meaning payment is due 30 days after the
invoice date.

### The Direct Connection to Cash Flow

Ask yourself: how does offering "Net 60" terms instead of "Net 30"
affect a business's cash flow, even on an identical sale? Longer payment
terms mean waiting longer to actually receive cash, widening exactly the
kind of timing gap covered in the cash flow lesson — a real, controllable
factor in a business's own cash position.

## Worked Example

A business offers Net 30 terms to a new client and Net 60 to a larger,
long-standing client who negotiated better terms. Ask yourself: does this
create different cash flow timing for identical-sized sales? Yes — the
Net 60 client's payment arrives a full month later, meaning the business
must cover its own expenses for that much longer before collecting.

## Practice Quiz

1. An invoice is:
   A. A type of business license
   B. A formal request for payment detailing what's owed
   C. A company's annual financial statement
   D. A marketing document

2. True or False: "Net 30" means payment is due immediately upon receipt
   of the invoice.

3. How do longer payment terms affect a business's cash flow?
   A. They have no effect at all
   B. They widen the timing gap before cash is actually received
   C. They guarantee faster payment
   D. They eliminate the need for invoicing

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. An invoice is a formal payment request.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. "Net 30" means payment is due 30 days after the
invoice date, not immediately.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Longer terms widen the cash flow timing gap.

## Key Takeaways

- An invoice is a formal request for payment.
- Payment terms specify when payment is actually due.
- Longer payment terms directly widen a business's cash flow timing
  gap.
