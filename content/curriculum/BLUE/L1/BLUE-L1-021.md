## Introduction

Every business owner should plan their eventual exit. This lesson covers the primary exit paths — sale, merger, acquisition, management buyout, and IPO — and explains how to maximize business value before exit.

This lesson builds on the foundations established in earlier Business lessons and advances your practical competency in this area.

## Core Content

The following principles form the foundation of this lesson. Each concept builds on the previous, so work through them in order before moving to the application exercises.

### Concept 1 — Foundation

Every practitioner must understand the fundamental principles that govern this area. These principles do not change with market conditions or economic cycles. They apply consistently and reliably.

### Concept 2 — Application

Knowing a principle is only the beginning. This section connects the theory to real-world application, showing how practitioners actually use these ideas in practice.
