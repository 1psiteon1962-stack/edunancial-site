## Introduction

Franchising offers a proven business model with built-in brand recognition and support. This lesson explains how franchise agreements work, how to evaluate a franchise opportunity, and the economics of franchising from both sides.

This lesson builds on the foundations established in earlier Business lessons and advances your practical competency in this area.
