## BLUE-L2-033
### Working With an Attorney: When and Why

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Identify key business situations where attorney involvement is particularly valuable.
- Explain the general benefit of establishing an ongoing attorney relationship, echoing the RED-track discussion.
- Reinforce the "business is a team sport" theme in a legal context.

**2. Core Content**
Echoing RED-L2-046's discussion of ongoing real estate attorney relationships, this lesson addresses when business attorney involvement proves particularly valuable: entity formation and structuring (Lessons 2–3), reviewing significant contracts (Lesson 29), addressing worker classification questions (Lesson 23), navigating potential disputes with vendors, customers, or employees, and planning for significant business changes such as bringing on a partner, seeking outside investment (Lesson 6), or planning an eventual exit (Lesson 41).

As with the real estate attorney discussion, building an ongoing relationship with a single business attorney, rather than engaging a different attorney for each isolated need, can provide more consistent, informed guidance as the business grows and its needs evolve, since that attorney develops familiarity with the specific business's history, structure, and goals over time.

**3. Worked Example**
Rather than searching for a new attorney each time a legal question arises, Marisol builds an ongoing relationship with a single business attorney from her initial entity formation onward, engaging the same attorney for her vendor agreements, her worker classification questions as she began hiring, and eventually for a significant new equipment financing agreement. This ongoing relationship allows her attorney to provide more informed, contextual guidance each time, already familiar with her business's history and goals, rather than needing to be brought up to speed from scratch with each new legal question.

**4. Practice Quiz**
1. Which of the following is a business situation where attorney involvement is particularly valuable?
   a) Entity formation and significant contract review
   b) Choosing a paint color for the storefront
   c) Deciding on a lunch menu
   d) Selecting a font for marketing materials
2. What benefit does an ongoing attorney relationship provide, echoing the RED-track discussion?
   a) No benefit compared to a new attorney each time
   b) More consistent, informed guidance from familiarity with the business's history and goals
   c) Guaranteed favorable legal outcomes
   d) Elimination of all legal costs
3. True or False: Engaging a different attorney for each isolated legal need always provides equally informed guidance as an ongoing relationship.
4. Why did Marisol build an ongoing relationship with a single business attorney?
   a) She wanted a new attorney for every single question
   b) It provided more consistent, contextual guidance as her business grew and its needs evolved
   c) It was required by law
   d) It eliminated the need for any legal fees

**5. Answer Key**
1. a  2. b  3. False  4. b

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. Entity formation and significant contract review are examples of situations where attorney involvement is particularly valuable.
2. An ongoing relationship provides more consistent, informed guidance from an attorney familiar with the business's history and goals.
3. An ongoing relationship generally provides more informed, contextual guidance than starting fresh with a new attorney each time.
4. Marisol's ongoing relationship allowed for more consistent, contextual guidance as her business's needs evolved over time.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- Attorney involvement is particularly valuable for entity formation, significant contracts, worker classification, and major business changes.
- This echoes the RED-track discussion of the value of an ongoing attorney relationship.
- A single, ongoing relationship provides more consistent, contextual guidance than repeated one-off engagements.
- This reinforces the "business is a team sport" theme in a specifically legal context.
