## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

- Identify the core concepts covered in The Time Value of Money
- Apply foundational principles from this lesson to real-world financial decisions
- Explain the key ideas to others in plain language

## Introduction

A dollar today is worth more than a dollar in the future. This lesson explains the time value of money, present and future value calculations, and why every financial decision should be evaluated through this lens.

This lesson introduces foundational Investing concepts that will be developed further through the curriculum.

## Core Content

### Concept 1 — Foundation

Every practitioner must understand the fundamental principles that govern this area. These principles do not change with market conditions or economic cycles. They apply consistently and reliably.

### Concept 2 — Application

Knowing a principle is only the beginning. This section connects the theory to real-world application, showing how practitioners actually use these ideas in practice.

### Concept 3 — Common Mistakes

Understanding what works is only half the education. Recognizing and avoiding the most common mistakes accelerates competency and prevents costly errors.

## Practical Application

Apply what you have learned by working through the following exercises:

1. Identify one example from your own life where this lesson's concepts currently apply or will apply in the future.
2. Write a one-paragraph explanation of this lesson's main idea as if explaining it to someone who has never studied Investing.
3. List three actions you could take in the next 30 days that reflect the principles in this lesson.
