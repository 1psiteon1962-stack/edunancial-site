# Preparing for Level 2: What's Next in Investing

## Learning Objectives
- Summarize the foundation Level 1 has established
- Preview the general direction Level 2 will build toward
- Recognize how foundational concepts will extend into more advanced material

## Core Content

Level 1 of the GOLD track has built a foundation using precious metals as a concrete, tangible anchor: the four functions of money, what investing actually means, dollar-cost averaging, compounding, risk tolerance and time horizon, diversification, liquidity, inflation, and the psychology of staying disciplined through price fluctuations. These aren't metals-specific concepts — they're general investing principles that happened to be easiest to teach clearly through a simple, tangible asset.

Level 2 will build on this foundation by extending into broader investment strategy and alternative assets beyond precious metals and beyond the paper assets covered in the WHITE track — portfolio construction across multiple asset types, more advanced risk management, and how the principles learned here (DCA, compounding, diversification, time horizon matching) apply when combining precious metals with other holdings into a more complete overall strategy.

## Worked Example

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE: A student who's internalized dollar-cost averaging and time horizon matching through the precious metals case study in this track will find those same concepts directly transferable when Level 2 introduces more complex portfolio construction — the underlying logic of consistent investing and matching risk to timeline doesn't change just because more asset types are involved. Level 1's foundation is designed specifically to make that transition feel like an extension of familiar principles, not an entirely new set of ideas.

## Key Takeaways
- Level 1 built a foundation of broadly applicable investing principles, taught through precious metals as a tangible anchor asset.
- These principles aren't metals-specific — they transfer directly to broader investing contexts.
- Level 2 will extend this foundation into portfolio construction, alternative assets, and more advanced risk management.
