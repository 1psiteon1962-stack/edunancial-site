## GOLD-L2-001
### Wealth Building as a Discipline: The First Interaction Between Pieces

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Explain how a single wealth-building decision touches multiple pieces at once, echoing every prior color's opening lesson.
- Identify at least three ways wealth-building decisions interact with other financial areas.
- Apply Level 2 "what else does this move affect" thinking to a wealth-building scenario.

**2. Core Content**
Level 1 introduced individual wealth-building concepts — net worth, assets, liabilities, saving — as separate pieces. Level 2 begins, as it has in every color, by watching what happens to the rest of the board when a wealth-building decision is made.

Consider deciding to increase monthly retirement contributions. This single move touches several pieces at once: cash flow (less take-home pay available for current spending, echoing GREEN-L2-002's gross-to-net discussion), taxes (potentially reducing current taxable income if using a traditional account, echoing GREEN-L2-025), asset allocation (the specific investments chosen within that account, echoing WHITE-L2-016), and time horizon (the multi-decade runway before the funds are needed, echoing WHITE-L2-027). None of these effects are automatically good or bad — they are pieces that now interact differently.

GOLD-L2 sits at a unique position in this curriculum: it is the color most explicitly about integrating decisions across every other color into a coherent wealth-building strategy, rather than focusing on a single asset class or domain. This lesson opens by naming that integrative role directly.

**3. Worked Example**
Considering increasing his retirement contribution rate, Devon (echoing GREEN-L2-003) maps the pieces before acting: his current take-home pay would decrease somewhat, his taxable income would decrease if using a traditional account (interacting with his marginal rate discussion from GREEN-L2), his overall asset allocation would need revisiting to ensure the additional contribution fits his target mix (echoing WHITE-L2-016), and his already-long time horizon supports a more aggressive allocation for these particular funds. Rather than treating this as a simple "save more" decision, Devon recognizes it touches cash flow, taxes, allocation, and time horizon simultaneously.

   d) Retirement contributions never affect taxes
4. What is the core Level 2 skill this lesson builds, echoing every prior color's opening lesson?
   a) Memorizing a single investment formula
   b) Asking "what else does this move affect?" before acting
   c) Avoiding all wealth-building decisions entirely
   d) Ignoring cash flow effects

**5. Answer Key**
1. c  2. False  3. b  4. b

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. Favorite vacation destination has no financial relationship to this decision; the other three are directly interacting pieces.
2. GOLD-L2 is explicitly positioned as the integrative color, combining decisions across every other color into coherent wealth-building strategy.
3. Devon's additional contribution needed to fit within his overall target allocation, directly echoing the WHITE-track discussion.
4. This lesson reinforces the same multi-piece thinking habit built throughout every prior color's Level 2.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- A wealth-building decision, like increasing retirement contributions, touches cash flow, taxes, allocation, and time horizon simultaneously.
- GOLD-L2 occupies a unique, integrative position in this curriculum, combining concepts across every other color.
- None of these effects are automatically good or bad — they are pieces that now interact differently.
- This lesson reinforces the same multi-piece thinking habit built throughout every prior color's Level 2.
