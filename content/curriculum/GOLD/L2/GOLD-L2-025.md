## GOLD-L2-025
### Tracking Progress: Why Net Worth Should Be Reviewed, Not Obsessed Over

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Explain a reasonable cadence for reviewing net worth progress.
- Distinguish healthy periodic review from unhealthy, excessive monitoring.
- Connect this concept to WHITE-L2-045's behavioral discipline discussion.

**2. Core Content**
Building directly on Lesson 24's practical net worth statement exercise, this lesson addresses how often and how to review that statement going forward. A commonly suggested reasonable cadence is reviewing net worth periodically — such as quarterly or annually — rather than daily or even monthly, since short-term fluctuations (particularly from paper asset volatility, echoing WHITE-L2-026's volatility discussion) can create misleading noise that obscures genuine longer-term progress.

This directly echoes WHITE-L2-045's behavioral discipline discussion: excessive, frequent monitoring of net worth can trigger the same fear-driven, reactive behaviors that excessive market-watching can trigger, potentially undermining the long-term, patient strategy this level has built throughout. A reasonable periodic review, by contrast, provides enough distance to see genuine trends while still catching any issues (such as an unexpected asset concentration developing, echoing Lesson 9) worth addressing before they become significant problems.

**3. Worked Example**
After initially checking her net worth calculation obsessively, sometimes daily, and finding herself anxious over short-term paper asset fluctuations that had no real bearing on her long-term progress, a learner shifts to a quarterly review cadence instead, echoing WHITE-L2-045's behavioral discipline discussion. This less frequent but still regular review allows her to see genuine longer-term trends in her net worth progress, rather than reacting emotionally to short-term noise that would have been essentially invisible at the quarterly review interval she now uses.

**5. Answer Key**
1. b  2. b  3. False  4. b

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. A periodic cadence, such as quarterly or annually, is commonly suggested rather than daily or even monthly review.
2. Frequent monitoring exposes short-term fluctuations that can trigger the same fear-driven behaviors discussed in WHITE-L2-045.
3. A periodic review cadence still allows catching emerging issues like asset concentration, while avoiding excessive short-term noise.
4. The learner's shift to quarterly reviews reduced her exposure to misleading short-term noise while still tracking genuine long-term progress.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- A commonly suggested net worth review cadence is quarterly or annually, rather than daily or even monthly.
- Excessive, frequent monitoring can trigger the same fear-driven behaviors WHITE-L2-045 discussed regarding market volatility.
- A reasonable periodic cadence still allows catching emerging issues, like asset concentration, before they become significant.
- This connects directly to WHITE-L2-045's behavioral discipline discussion, applied specifically to net worth tracking.
