## GOLD-L2-031
### Due Diligence Revisited: A GOLD Perspective Across Asset Classes

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Reinforce the RED-L2-030 due diligence discussion at the integrative, cross-asset-class level.
- Apply due diligence principles to business equity and paper asset opportunities.
- Explain why due diligence rigor should not vary based on how exciting an opportunity feels.

**2. Core Content**
Revisiting RED-L2-030's due diligence discussion, this lesson extends the same underlying discipline across the full range of asset classes this level integrates. Just as a real estate buyer investigates physical condition, title, and environmental factors before finalizing a purchase, a business equity investor should investigate financial statements (echoing BLUE-L2-044–045), legal structure (echoing PURPLE-L2), and the specific terms of any partnership or syndication agreement (echoing BLUE-L2-029), while a paper asset investor should review a fund's actual prospectus (echoing WHITE-L2-041) rather than relying on marketing materials alone.

A common, costly failure pattern this lesson specifically warns against: allowing genuine excitement about an opportunity's potential to reduce due diligence rigor, precisely when that excitement should instead prompt more careful scrutiny, not less, echoing RED-L2-030's Nadia example of resisting the temptation to waive protective diligence steps under competitive or emotional pressure.

**3. Worked Example**
Excited about a promising business equity investment opportunity, a learner recognizes her own enthusiasm as a signal to apply more careful due diligence, not less, echoing RED-L2-030's lesson. She reviews the business's actual financial statements, confirms the legal structure of her proposed ownership stake with an attorney, and verifies the specific terms of the partnership agreement, rather than allowing her genuine excitement about the opportunity's potential to substitute for this careful, deliberate investigation process.
