## GOLD-L2-049
### Reviewing the Whole Board: Integrating RED, WHITE, BLUE, GREEN, and GOLD

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Synthesize the cross-color connections identified throughout GOLD-L2.
- Explain how each color's Level 2 concepts feed into this level's integrative framework.
- Reinforce the curriculum's intentional cross-color design at its most comprehensive point.

**2. Core Content**
This lesson consolidates the cross-color threads woven throughout GOLD-L2, reviewing "the whole board" this curriculum has built across five colors so far. RED-L2's real estate concepts (leverage, cash flow, cycles) feed directly into this level's asset class comparison (Lesson 21) and risk-return principles (Lesson 7). WHITE-L2's paper asset concepts (diversification, correlation, behavioral discipline) feed directly into Lessons 9–10, 22, and 41. BLUE-L2's business concepts (equity, valuation, growth) feed directly into Lesson 20 and the multi-asset strategy synthesis (Lesson 23). GREEN-L2's tax concepts feed into every wealth-building decision's after-tax reality, echoing GREEN-L2-023's own preview of this exact connection. PURPLE-L2's legal instruments feed directly into Lesson 40's wealth transfer discussion.

This lesson makes explicit what GOLD-L2 has embodied throughout: this color is not simply another asset-class-specific track alongside the others, but the integrative capstone bringing every other color's Level 2 concepts together into a coherent, comprehensive wealth-building competency — precisely the "how pieces work together" theme this entire Level 2, across every color, has built toward.

**3. Worked Example**
Reflecting on everything covered across RED-L2, WHITE-L2, BLUE-L2, GREEN-L2, PURPLE-L2, and now GOLD-L2, a learner maps how each color's concepts fed into her comprehensive wealth-building framework: real estate leverage and cash flow, paper asset diversification and behavioral discipline, business equity and valuation, tax mechanics shaping every after-tax decision, and legal instruments enabling eventual wealth transfer. This mapping exercise helps her see GOLD-L2 not as a separate, isolated 50-lesson unit, but as the culminating integration point for the wealth-building competency this curriculum has built across every color so far.
