## Core Content
Extending this curriculum's earlier WHITE-L5, BLUE-L5, and GREEN-L5 trust structuring discussions, VERIFIED FACT, the same general control-versus-protection framework those discussions established applies with full force to physical precious metals and alternative asset holdings as well, meaning a revocable trust holding these physical assets generally allows the grantor to retain considerable control but provides more limited genuine asset protection, while an irrevocable trust can provide meaningfully stronger protection precisely because the grantor has genuinely, permanently relinquished this degree of ongoing control.

This is why the same genuine control-versus-protection tradeoff applies with full force to physical alternative assets as well: precisely echoing this curriculum's parallel trust structuring discussions across paper assets, business ownership, and general tax planning, this same fundamental tradeoff between genuine control retention and genuine asset protection strength applies without meaningful distinction to physical alternative asset trust structuring, meaning a family should specifically apply this same, now-familiar framework to this specific asset category. This is why a family should orchestrate their own genuine, honest priorities regarding control, asset protection, and tax treatment together: precisely echoing this curriculum's recurring finding across multiple asset categories, a family should specifically, honestly orchestrate their own genuine priorities across all three of these considerations together when structuring a trust for physical alternative assets, since the specific trust structure a family ultimately selects will typically determine all three of these outcomes simultaneously, rather than choosing based on tax treatment alone without regard to the genuinely linked control and protection consequences this lesson has described.
