## Introduction

Above-the-line deductions reduce your adjusted gross income before the standard deduction is applied. This lesson covers student loan interest, IRA contributions, self-employment taxes, and other above-the-line deductions available to taxpayers.

This lesson introduces foundational Taxes concepts that will be developed further through the curriculum.
