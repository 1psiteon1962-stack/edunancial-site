## GREEN-L2-021
### How Taxes Interact With Real Estate (GREEN Meets RED)

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Reinforce the RED-track tax touchpoints from the GREEN tax perspective.
- Identify at least four real estate decisions with direct tax implications.
- Recognize the consistent professional-guidance theme across both tracks.

**2. Core Content**
This lesson revisits, from the GREEN perspective, several real estate tax touchpoints already introduced throughout RED-L2: property tax assessment (RED-L2-014), depreciation (RED-L2-015), 1031 exchanges (RED-L2-016), and the entity-tax interaction discussed in RED-L2-013. Each of these represents a point where a real estate decision directly triggers tax consequences, reinforcing this level's broader theme that no color's decisions exist in true isolation from GREEN-track tax considerations.

Consistent with RED-L2-045's explicit deferral of specific tax numbers to professionals, this lesson does not attempt to provide specific calculations for any of these interactions, since real estate tax treatment is highly individualized and jurisdiction-specific. Instead, this lesson reinforces the pattern: recognizing when a real estate decision has crossed into tax territory significant enough to warrant professional consultation, echoing the exact same conclusion RED-L2-045 reached from the real estate side of this same cross-color relationship.

**3. Worked Example**
Planning to sell a rental property she has owned for years, Fatima recognizes several tax touchpoints simultaneously: her property's depreciation history (echoing RED-L2-015) will affect her tax treatment upon sale, and she is considering whether a 1031 exchange (RED-L2-016) might allow her to defer certain tax consequences by reinvesting in another property. Rather than attempting to calculate these effects herself from general education alone, she schedules a comprehensive meeting with her tax professional well before her planned sale, allowing enough time to properly evaluate her options — directly mirroring RED-L2-045's Andre example from the opposite direction.

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. Depreciation and 1031 exchanges are RED-track topics with direct tax implications discussed in this lesson.
2. Both this lesson and RED-L2-045 emphasize recognizing when to bring in a tax professional, rather than attempting a do-it-yourself calculation.
3. This lesson deliberately avoids specific tax calculations, consistent with this curriculum's professional-guidance theme.
4. Fatima's advance scheduling allowed adequate time for her tax professional to properly evaluate her multiple, interacting tax touchpoints.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- Real estate decisions like property tax assessment, depreciation, and 1031 exchanges all carry direct tax implications.
- This lesson reinforces RED-L2-045's theme from the GREEN perspective: recognize the tax touchpoint, then consult a professional.
- Real estate tax treatment is highly individualized and jurisdiction-specific, requiring professional guidance, not general assumptions.
- This cross-color connection illustrates that no color's decisions exist in true isolation from GREEN-track tax considerations.
