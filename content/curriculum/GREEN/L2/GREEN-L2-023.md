## GREEN-L2-023
### How Taxes Interact With Investing (GREEN Meets WHITE and GOLD)

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Reinforce the WHITE-track tax touchpoints from the GREEN tax perspective.
- Distinguish taxable and tax-advantaged investment accounts.
- Preview the GOLD-track connection to broader wealth-building tax strategy.

**2. Core Content**
This lesson revisits, from the GREEN perspective, several investing tax touchpoints introduced throughout WHITE-L2: capital gains treatment (WHITE-L2-031), tax-advantaged accounts (WHITE-L2-029), and asset location strategy (WHITE-L2-030). Capital gains — the profit from selling an investment above its purchase price — are commonly subject to different tax treatment depending on how long the investment was held (echoing the short-term versus long-term distinction from WHITE-L2-031), while tax-advantaged accounts allow certain investment growth to occur with reduced or deferred current taxation, depending on the specific account type and jurisdiction.

This connection extends naturally toward the GOLD track (Investing/Wealth Building) as well: broader wealth-building strategy inherently involves tax considerations at every stage — accumulation (which account types and investment choices minimize unnecessary current taxation), and eventual distribution (how withdrawals or sales will be taxed) — making GREEN-track tax literacy a genuine prerequisite for effective GOLD-track wealth-building strategy, not a separate, unrelated consideration.

**3. Worked Example**
Building her long-term investment portfolio, Simone (echoing WHITE-L2-030's asset location discussion) works with her financial advisor to place her higher-taxable-income investments within her tax-advantaged retirement account, while holding her long-term stock index investments within her taxable brokerage account, given their generally more favorable long-term capital gains treatment there. She recognizes this coordinated approach reflects the same GREEN-track tax literacy that will remain relevant as she progresses into more advanced GOLD-track wealth-building strategy in later levels.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- Capital gains treatment and tax-advantaged accounts are key WHITE-track topics with direct GREEN-track tax implications.
- Tax-advantaged accounts allow reduced or deferred current taxation on investment growth, depending on account type.
- This connection extends naturally to GOLD-track wealth-building strategy, which involves tax considerations throughout.
- GREEN-track tax literacy is a genuine prerequisite for effective wealth-building strategy, not a separate consideration.
