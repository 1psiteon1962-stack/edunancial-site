## GREEN-L2-050
### Looking Ahead: Tax Strategy and Integrated Planning (Bridge to Level 3)

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Summarize the overall progression of GREEN-L2 from Lesson 1 to Lesson 49.
- Explain the general shift from individual tax mechanics to integrated, multi-year tax strategy.
- Preview the direction of GREEN-L3.

**2. Core Content**
GREEN-L2 began with the first interaction between pieces in a single paycheck's journey from gross to net, then built outward through tax mechanics — brackets, rates, filing status, deductions and credits (Lessons 2–8), withholding and self-employment tax (Lessons 9–14), budgeting and debt strategy (Lessons 15–20), cross-color tax touchpoints (Lessons 21–24), tax-advantaged accounts and dependent/education benefits (Lessons 25–29), the state-versus-federal and multi-tax-system landscape (Lessons 30–32), practical tax administration (Lessons 33–38), the planning-versus-preparation distinction (Lesson 39), specialized topics like charitable giving, capital gains, and estate tax (Lessons 40–42), life events and modern complications (Lessons 43–44), critical legal-versus-illegal distinctions (Lesson 45), and practical organizational and decision frameworks (Lessons 46–49).

This final lesson previews GREEN-L3's direction: moving from understanding individual tax mechanics and rules (Level 2) toward increasingly realistic, integrated, multi-year tax strategy — echoing this curriculum's broader progression, GREEN-L3 will likely explore how tax planning decisions made in one year affect subsequent years, how tax strategy integrates with the broader wealth-building decisions previewed in Lesson 23's GOLD-track connection, and increasingly realistic scenarios combining multiple GREEN-L2 concepts simultaneously, moving the learner from "how pieces interact" (Level 2) toward "using combinations in increasingly realistic decisions" (Level 3).

**3. Worked Example**
Reflecting on everything covered in GREEN-L2, a learner reviews their own growing tax literacy: they began confused by a single paycheck's gap between stated salary and actual take-home pay, and they now understand marginal versus effective rates, deductions versus credits, self-employment tax, budgeting integration, and the critical distinction between legitimate tax avoidance and illegal evasion. This learner recognizes that GREEN-L3 will build on this foundation by combining these individual mechanics into more complex, multi-year, integrated tax strategy, just as RED-L3, WHITE-L3, BLUE-L3, and PURPLE-L3 build on their respective Level 2 foundations.
