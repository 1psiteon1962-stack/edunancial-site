## Core Content
Extending this curriculum's earlier RED-L5, WHITE-L5, and BLUE-L5 exit strategy discussions, VERIFIED FACT, the same orchestrated framework those lessons applied to real estate, paper asset, and business exit timing — coordinating sale timing, tax consequences, and reinvestment planning together — applies with equal force to the tax planning dimension of any significant asset sale, meaning a taxpayer should orchestrate these same categories of considerations rather than treating a significant sale's tax consequences as a simple, single, isolated calculation.

Here is the "why" genuine, complete tax consequences should be orchestrated into the sale timing decision itself, connecting to this level's earlier discussions: echoing this level's earlier Lesson 2 and Lesson 9 discussions regarding marginal rate timing, Lesson 17's discussion regarding holding period requirements, and Lesson 11's discussion regarding depreciation recapture, a taxpayer planning a significant sale should specifically orchestrate all of these genuine tax consequence dimensions together — confirming the applicable marginal rate at the time of sale, confirming the holding period threshold has genuinely been met for favorable treatment, and confirming any applicable depreciation recapture liability — rather than considering any single one of these dimensions in isolation from the others. This is why a genuine reinvestment plan should be orchestrated into the exit decision from the outset, connecting to this level's earlier Lesson 29 discussion: echoing that discussion's emphasis on genuine planning flexibility amid legislative uncertainty, a taxpayer should specifically have a genuine, flexible plan for how sale proceeds will actually be reinvested and how this reinvestment interacts with the taxpayer's own broader, ongoing tax situation, rather than exiting a position first and only subsequently considering these genuine tax planning implications after the fact. This is why this lesson synthesizes several of this level's earlier discussions into a single, coherent exit tax framework: drawing together this level's earlier marginal rate timing, holding period, and depreciation recapture discussions, this lesson demonstrates precisely the kind of integrated, multi-concept orchestration this level's opening Lesson 1 discussion identified as its central, defining purpose, now applied specifically to the concrete, practical tax planning decisions surrounding a significant asset sale.
