## ORANGE-L2-010
### Target Market vs. Target Audience: A Comparison

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Distinguish target market from target audience.
- Explain why these related concepts serve different practical purposes.
- Apply this comparison to a hypothetical marketing scenario.

**2. Core Content**
Target market generally refers to the broader group of customers a business's product or service is designed to serve — defined by characteristics like need, demographics, or industry. Target audience generally refers to the more specific group a particular marketing message or campaign is designed to reach — which may be narrower than, or a specific subset of, the overall target market, and can shift from campaign to campaign even while the overall target market remains constant.

This distinction matters practically: a business's target market might be broadly defined (small business owners needing accounting software), while a specific advertising campaign's target audience might be a narrower subset of that market (small business owners in a specific industry, or at a specific business stage) — recognizing this distinction helps a business tailor specific campaigns appropriately without needing every single campaign to address its entire, broader target market simultaneously.

**3. Worked Example**
Marketing her accounting software, a business owner defines her overall target market broadly as small business owners across various industries needing simplified bookkeeping. For a specific advertising campaign, however, she narrows her target audience to specifically address freelance graphic designers, a subset of her broader target market with particular, well-understood pain points her campaign messaging can address more specifically and effectively than a message trying to speak to her entire, broader target market at once.

**4. Practice Quiz**
1. What does target market generally refer to?
   a) The specific audience for a single advertising campaign only
   b) The broader group of customers a business's product or service is designed to serve
   c) A type of tax credit
   d) A specific sales technique
2. What does target audience generally refer to, as distinguished from target market?
   a) An identical concept to target market with no distinction
   b) The more specific group a particular marketing message or campaign is designed to reach, which may be a subset of the target market
   c) Only the business's existing customers
   d) A concept unrelated to marketing campaigns
3. True or False: Every specific marketing campaign must always address a business's entire, broader target market simultaneously.
4. Why did the business owner narrow her target audience to freelance graphic designers for a specific campaign?
   a) She wanted to abandon her broader target market entirely
   b) This narrower subset allowed more specific, effective messaging addressing well-understood pain points than a broader message would
   c) Target audience and target market are identical concepts requiring no distinction
   d) She had no broader target market to consider

**5. Answer Key**
1. b  2. b  3. False  4. b

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. Target market generally refers to the broader group of customers a business's product or service is designed to serve.
2. Target audience generally refers to the more specific group a particular campaign is designed to reach, potentially a subset of the target market.
3. A specific campaign can effectively address a narrower subset of the broader target market, rather than the entire market simultaneously.
4. The business owner's narrower target audience allowed more specific, effective messaging for that particular campaign.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- Target market is the broader group a business's product or service is designed to serve.
- Target audience is the more specific group a particular campaign is designed to reach, potentially a subset of the target market.
- A business's target market can remain constant while specific campaigns' target audiences shift.
- This distinction helps tailor specific campaigns effectively without requiring every campaign to address the entire broader market.
