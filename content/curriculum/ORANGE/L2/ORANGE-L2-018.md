## ORANGE-L2-018
### Social Media Marketing: Platform Fit Over Platform Presence

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Explain why platform selection should be driven by target audience fit, not general presence.
- Identify general considerations for evaluating platform fit.
- Apply this concept to a hypothetical platform selection scenario.

**2. Core Content**
Building on Lesson 10's target audience discussion, this lesson addresses a common, costly mistake: attempting to maintain a strong presence on every available social media platform simultaneously, regardless of whether a business's specific target audience actually spends meaningful time there. Different platforms generally attract different demographic and psychographic audiences, and different platforms suit different content formats and business types — a platform well-suited to one business's specific audience and content style may be poorly suited to another's, even within a broadly similar industry.

Rather than spreading limited marketing resources thinly across every platform, a more effective approach generally involves identifying which specific platform or platforms genuinely align with a business's target audience (echoing Lesson 12's persona research) and content strengths, then investing more deeply and consistently in that focused selection, rather than maintaining a superficial, under-resourced presence across many platforms simultaneously.

**3. Worked Example**
Rather than attempting to maintain an active presence across every major social media platform, a business owner researches where her specific target persona (echoing Lesson 12) actually spends meaningful time and engages with relevant content, discovering a strong concentration on one particular platform well-suited to her visual product content. She focuses her limited marketing resources deeply on that specific platform, producing more frequent, higher-quality content there than a spread-thin, superficial presence across many platforms would have allowed.

**4. Practice Quiz**
1. What common, costly mistake does this lesson identify regarding social media marketing?
   a) Focusing deeply on a single platform genuinely suited to the target audience
   b) Attempting to maintain a strong presence on every available platform simultaneously, regardless of actual audience fit
   c) Researching where the target audience actually spends time
   d) Avoiding social media marketing entirely
2. What should drive platform selection, per this lesson?
   a) Simply using every platform available, regardless of audience fit
   b) Genuine alignment with the target audience and content strengths, informed by persona research
   c) Platform selection should be entirely random
   d) Only the newest, most trending platform should ever be used
3. True or False: Every business should maintain an equally strong, deeply resourced presence across every available social media platform.
4. Why did the business owner focus her resources on a single platform rather than spreading across many?
   a) She had no target audience to consider
   b) Her research revealed a strong concentration of her target persona on that specific platform, allowing deeper, higher-quality investment there
   c) Spreading resources thinly across many platforms always produces better results
   d) Platform fit has no relationship to marketing effectiveness

**5. Answer Key**
1. b  2. b  3. False  4. b

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. Attempting to maintain a strong presence on every platform simultaneously, regardless of actual audience fit, is identified as a common, costly mistake.
2. Platform selection should be driven by genuine alignment with the target audience and content strengths.
3. Focused investment in platforms genuinely suited to the target audience is generally more effective than a spread-thin, superficial presence everywhere.
4. The business owner's research-informed focus on a single well-suited platform allowed deeper, more effective investment than a spread-thin approach.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- Platform selection should be driven by genuine target audience fit, not an attempt at universal presence.
- Different platforms attract different audiences and suit different content formats and business types.
- Focused, deep investment in a well-suited platform generally outperforms a spread-thin presence across many platforms.
- This builds directly on Lesson 10's target audience discussion and Lesson 12's persona research.
