## ORANGE-L2-029
### Truth in Advertising Revisited (ORANGE Meets PURPLE)

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Reinforce PURPLE-L2-041's advertising law discussion from the ORANGE marketing perspective.
- Apply this legal framework to the pricing psychology and persuasion ethics discussed in Lessons 23 and 28.
- Explain why legal compliance and ethical practice, while related, are not identical.

**2. Core Content**
Revisiting PURPLE-L2-041's discussion of truthful, substantiated advertising claims, this lesson examines how those legal requirements interact directly with the pricing psychology (Lesson 23) and persuasion ethics (Lesson 28) discussed throughout this level. An anchored reference price that was never genuinely offered, for example, does not merely raise the ethical concern previewed in Lesson 23 — it may also constitute the kind of misleading advertising claim PURPLE-L2-041 addresses as a genuine legal compliance matter, independent of the ethical dimension.

This lesson emphasizes that legal compliance and ethical practice, while closely related and often overlapping, are not identical: a specific marketing claim might be technically legally compliant while still falling short of the genuine, relationship-preserving ethical standard Lesson 28 described, meaning a responsible business should treat legal compliance as a necessary minimum floor, not a complete, sufficient ethical standard on its own.

**3. Worked Example**
Reviewing her marketing materials with both legal and ethical considerations in mind, a business owner ensures her pricing claims and product performance statements meet PURPLE-L2-041's substantiation requirements as a genuine legal compliance matter, while also applying Lesson 28's higher ethical bar — asking not just "is this technically legally compliant" but "would this genuinely serve my customers' honest understanding of what they're purchasing." She recognizes these as related but distinct standards, treating legal compliance as a necessary floor rather than assuming it alone fully satisfies her ethical obligations to her customers.
