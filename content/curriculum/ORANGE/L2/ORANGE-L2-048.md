## ORANGE-L2-048
### When to Hire a Marketing Professional vs. Do It Yourself

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Reinforce the "business is a team sport" theme applied specifically to marketing.
- Identify general factors suggesting when professional marketing help becomes valuable.
- Apply this concept to a hypothetical growth-stage scenario.

**2. Core Content**
Echoing BLUE-L2-031's "business is a team sport" theme and GREEN-L2-038's software-versus-professional discussion, this lesson addresses when a business might benefit from professional marketing help rather than continuing to handle marketing entirely independently. General factors suggesting increasing value from professional guidance include growing complexity across multiple channels (echoing Lesson 21's channel selection discussion), insufficient internal time or expertise to properly execute the evidence-based testing and measurement (Lessons 38–40) this level has emphasized, and reaching a growth stage where marketing's potential impact on revenue justifies specialized expertise beyond what a business owner can reasonably develop and maintain personally.

As with the parallel discussions across other colors, this is not a rule that professional help is always necessary, nor that a business owner cannot effectively handle marketing personally, particularly in earlier stages with simpler needs; it is a spectrum where increasing complexity and stakes generally shift the balance toward professional guidance being worth its cost, echoing the same team-sport principle applied throughout this curriculum.

**3. Worked Example**
In her business's early stages, a business owner confidently handles her own marketing, given its relative simplicity and her genuine interest in learning the fundamentals this level has covered. As her business grows and her marketing needs expand across multiple channels with increasingly sophisticated testing and measurement requirements, she recognizes her situation has grown complex enough that bringing on specialized marketing help would likely be worth its cost, echoing BLUE-L2-031's team-sport theme, rather than continuing to stretch her own limited time and expertise across an increasingly complex marketing operation.

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. Growing complexity across multiple channels and insufficient time or expertise for evidence-based testing increase the value of professional guidance.
2. This lesson echoes BLUE-L2-031's theme, recognizing that increasing complexity and stakes shift the balance toward professional guidance being worth its cost.
3. The right choice depends on the business's specific situation complexity and growth stage, not a universal rule.
4. The business owner's growing complexity appropriately shifted the balance toward considering specialized marketing help.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- General factors favoring professional marketing help include growing channel complexity and insufficient time for proper testing and measurement.
- This echoes BLUE-L2-031's "business is a team sport" theme and GREEN-L2-038's software-versus-professional discussion.
- This is a spectrum, not a fixed rule, with the balance shifting as complexity and stakes grow.
- Recognizing when complexity warrants professional guidance is itself a valuable Level 2 skill.
