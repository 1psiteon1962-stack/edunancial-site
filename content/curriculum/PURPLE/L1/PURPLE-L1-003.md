## Introduction

The legal structure of a business determines how it is taxed, how liability is managed, and how ownership is transferred. This lesson covers sole proprietorships, LLCs, corporations, and partnerships from a legal perspective.

This lesson introduces foundational Law concepts that will be developed further through the curriculum.
