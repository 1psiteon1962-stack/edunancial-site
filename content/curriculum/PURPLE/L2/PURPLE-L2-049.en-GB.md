## PURPLE-L2-049
### Building a Personal and Business Legal Decision Framework

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Assemble a structured set of questions drawn from across this level into a legal decision framework.
- Apply this framework to a hypothetical legal decision.
- Explain how this framework embodies Level 2 "chess piece" thinking.

**2. Core Content**
Echoing the RED, WHITE, and BLUE-track decision frameworks, this lesson consolidates PURPLE-L2 into a practical legal decision framework, structured around questions drawn from earlier lessons: What type of legal instrument is involved, and does it meet the basic enforceability requirements (Lessons 2–7)? Does this situation call for a specialised instrument like an NDA, non-compete, will, trust, or power of attorney (Lessons 8–17)? What employment or intellectual property considerations apply (Lessons 18–25)? What liability, indemnification, or dispute resolution provisions are relevant (Lessons 26–31)? What underlying legal foundation — contract, tort, regulatory — actually governs this situation (Lessons 32–41)? And what governance, jurisdiction, or professional engagement questions does this decision raise (Lessons 42–48)?

As with the other colour frameworks, this is a habit of mind rather than a rigid checklist: deliberately tracing a legal question's effects across instrument type, specialised protections, liability, underlying legal foundation, and professional guidance, rather than treating any single legal document or question in isolation from the broader legal landscape this level has mapped.

**3. Worked Example**
Considering a new business partnership opportunity, a founder runs through this framework deliberately: confirming the partnership agreement itself meets basic enforceability requirements, considering whether an NDA or non-compete is needed given the sensitive information involved, addressing intellectual property ownership questions for work the partnership will produce, reviewing liability and indemnification provisions carefully, determining whether this relationship raises primarily contract law questions or also potential regulatory compliance issues specific to the industry, and confirming whether the partnership's structure raises governance questions requiring specific professional guidance before proceeding.

**4. Practice Quiz**
1. What is the purpose of the legal decision framework introduced in this lesson?
   a) To replace all professional legal advice
   b) To provide a structured habit of tracing a legal decision's effects across multiple pieces from this level
   c) To guarantee a favourable legal outcome
   d) To eliminate the need for any research
2. Which of the following is part of the framework?
   a) Instrument type/enforceability, specialised protections, liability/dispute resolution, underlying legal foundation, and professional guidance
   b) Only the document's page count
   c) Only the other party's personality
   d) Only the colour of the paper used
3. True or False: This framework is meant to be applied mechanically without genuine analysis of the specific situation.
4. What did the founder evaluate before proceeding with the new business partnership?
   a) Only the partnership's name
   b) Enforceability, specialised protections, IP ownership, liability, underlying legal foundation, and governance questions together
   c) Nothing — she proceeded without any analysis
   d) Only her personal preference

**5. Answer Key**
1. b  2. a  3. False  4. b

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. This framework builds the habit of comprehensively tracing a legal decision's effects across multiple pieces, not guaranteeing outcomes.
2. The framework spans instrument type/enforceability, specialised protections, liability/dispute resolution, legal foundation, and professional guidance.
3. The framework is meant to prompt genuine, situation-specific analysis, not mechanical box-checking.
4. The founder's systematic evaluation across multiple legal pieces replaced a decision based on a single document or question in isolation.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- This lesson consolidates PURPLE-L2 into a practical framework spanning instrument type, specialised protections, liability, legal foundation, and professional guidance.
- The framework is a habit of mind, echoing the RED, WHITE, and BLUE-track frameworks' structure.
- Applying it systematically helps avoid treating a single legal document or question in isolation.
- This framework embodies the comprehensive "chess piece" thinking this entire level has built toward.
