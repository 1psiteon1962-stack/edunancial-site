# What Is a Mortgage? (Not Just "A Loan for a House")

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define a mortgage in plain terms.
- Explain what makes a mortgage different from an unsecured loan.
- Describe what happens if a borrower stops paying.

## Core Content

### A Mortgage Is a Secured Loan

A mortgage is a loan used to buy real estate, where the property itself
serves as collateral. "Secured" means the lender has a legal claim on
the property if the borrower doesn't repay — this is different from an
unsecured loan (like most credit card debt), where the lender has no
specific asset to claim.

### Why the Security Matters

Because the loan is secured by the property, mortgages typically come
with lower interest rates than unsecured loans — the lender's risk is
reduced by having the property as backup. This is also why lenders
require an appraisal and insurance on the property before approving the
loan.

### What Happens If Payments Stop

If a borrower stops making mortgage payments, the lender can eventually
begin foreclosure — the legal process of taking the property to recover
what's owed. This is a last resort for lenders, not an immediate
reaction to a single missed payment, and is covered in more depth in a
later lesson.

## Worked Example

A borrower takes out a $250,000 mortgage to buy a home. The home itself
is the collateral. If the borrower stops paying and the lender
forecloses, the property can be sold to recover the unpaid balance.

## Practice Quiz

1. A mortgage is best described as:
   A. An unsecured personal loan
   B. A loan secured by the property being purchased
   C. A type of insurance
   D. A government grant

2. True or False: Mortgages typically have lower interest rates than
   unsecured loans because the property serves as collateral.

3. What is foreclosure?
   A. A type of mortgage insurance
   B. The legal process of a lender taking the property after failure to
      repay
   C. A tax on home purchases
   D. A required home inspection

## Answer Key

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. A mortgage is specifically a loan secured by the
property being purchased.

### Question 2

Correct answer: True. The collateral reduces the lender's risk, which
generally translates to a lower rate than unsecured debt.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Foreclosure is the legal process lenders use to
recover unpaid amounts by taking the secured property.

## Key Takeaways

- A mortgage is a loan secured by the real estate being purchased.
- The security (collateral) is why mortgage rates are typically lower
  than unsecured loans.
- Foreclosure is the legal consequence of failing to repay a mortgage.
