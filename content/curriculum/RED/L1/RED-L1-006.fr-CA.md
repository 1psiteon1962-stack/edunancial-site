# Qu’est-ce qu’une mise de fonds et pourquoi est-elle si importante?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a down payment in plain terms.
- Explain how the down payment size affects the prêt and monthly payment.
- Explain why prêteurs care about down payment size beyond the math.

## Contenu principal

### What a Down Payment Is

Une mise de fonds is the portion of a propriété's purchase price paid
upfront, in cash, rather than borrowed. If a home costs $300,000 and the
acheteur pays $30,000 upfront, that's a 10% down payment, with the
remaining $270,000 financed through a hypothèque.

### How It Affects the Prêt

A larger down payment means a smaller prêt amount, which generally means
a smaller monthly payment and less intérêts paid over the life of the
prêt. It directly changes the numbers, not just the acheteur's comfort
level.

### Why Lenders Care Beyond the Math

A larger down payment also means the acheteur already has real valeur nette (and
real money) in the propriété from day one, which reduces the prêteur's
risk — a acheteur with meaningful valeur nette has more to lose by defaulting.
This is part of why smaller down payments often come with additional
costs, like PMI, covered in the next lesson.

## Exemple pratique

A $300,000 home with a 20% down payment ($60,000) requires financing
$240,000. The same home with a 5% down payment ($15,000) requires
financing $285,000 — a larger prêt, larger monthly payment, and more
total intérêts paid over time.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Une mise de fonds is:
   A. A monthly fee paid to the prêteur
   B. The portion of the purchase price paid upfront in cash
   C. A type of home insurance
   D. A tax paid at closing

2. Vrai ou faux: A larger down payment generally results in a smaller
   monthly hypothèque payment.

3. Why do prêteurs generally view a larger down payment as lower risk?
   A. It guarantees the home's valeur will rise
   B. The acheteur has more of their own money and valeur nette at stake
   C. It eliminates the need for a credit check
   D. It shortens the prêt term automatically

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Une mise de fonds is the upfront cash portion of the
purchase price.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. A larger down payment reduces the prêt amount,
which reduces the monthly payment.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. More of the acheteur's own money at stake reduces the
prêteur's risk of default.

## Points clés

- Une mise de fonds is the upfront cash portion of a home purchase.
- A larger down payment means a smaller prêt, smaller payments, and less
  total intérêts.
- Lenders view larger down payments as lower risk because the acheteur has
  more at stake.
