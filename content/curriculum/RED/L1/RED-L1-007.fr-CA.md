# Hypothèques à taux fixe ou à taux variable

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a fixed-rate hypothèque and an adjustable-rate hypothèque.
- Explain the trade-off between predictability and potential savings.
- Identify one risk unique to adjustable-rate hypothèques.

## Contenu principal

### Fixed-Rate Hypothèques

A fixed-rate hypothèque locks in the same intérêts rate for the entire
prêt term. The monthly principal-and-intérêts payment stays the same
from the first payment to the last, making budgeting predictable.

### Adjustable-Rate Hypothèques (ARMs)

An adjustable-rate hypothèque starts with a rate that's often lower than a
comparable fixed rate, but that rate can change periodically after an
initial fixed period, based on broader intérêts rate conditions. Payments
can go up or down over time.

### The Real Trade-Off

Fixed-rate prêts trade a potentially higher starting rate for long-term
predictability. ARMs trade predictability for a potentially lower
starting cost — with real risk that payments could rise later. Neither
is universally better; it depends on how long someone plans to keep the
prêt and their tolerance for payment uncertainty.

## Exemple pratique

A fixed-rate borrower pays the same amount in year 1 and year 15. An ARM
borrower might pay less in year 1 than the fixed-rate borrower, but if
rates rise, could be paying more by year 6 — the exact opposite could
also happen if rates fall.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A fixed-rate hypothèque:
   A. Changes rate every year automatically
   B. Keeps the same intérêts rate for the entire prêt term
   C. Is only available to first-time acheteurs
   D. Always has a higher payment than an ARM

2. Vrai ou faux: An adjustable-rate hypothèque's payment can never
   increase after the initial period.

3. What is the main trade-off between fixed and adjustable-rate
   hypothèques?
   A. Predictability vs. potentially lower starting cost with risk of
      change
   B. Legal vs. illegal prêt types
   C. Government-backed vs. private prêts
   D. Short-term vs. long-term propriété propriétaireship

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. A

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A fixed-rate hypothèque keeps the same rate for the
full prêt term.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. ARM payments can rise (or fall) after the initial
fixed period, based on rate conditions.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: A. The core trade-off is long-term predictability versus
a potentially lower starting cost with future rate risk.

## Points clés

- Fixed-rate hypothèques offer a locked, predictable rate for the full
  term.
- ARMs often start lower but can change after an initial period.
- The right choice depends on how long someone plans to keep the prêt
  and their comfort with payment uncertainty.
