# Qu’est-ce que l’assurance hypothécaire privée (PMI) et quand la payez-vous?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define PMI in plain terms.
- Explain who PMI actually protects.
- Explain when PMI is typically required, and when it can go away.

## Contenu principal

### What PMI Is

Private Hypothèque Insurance (PMI) is insurance that protects the prêteur —
not the borrower — if the borrower stops paying the prêt. It's an
additional monthly cost added to the hypothèque payment.

### Who It Actually Protects

This is the most misunderstood part: PMI doesn't protect the homepropriétaire
from losing their home. It exists to reduce the prêteur's risk on prêts
where the acheteur has less valeur nette cushion, so the prêteur is more willing
to approve the prêt in the first place.

### When It's Required, and When It Ends

PMI is typically required on conventional prêts when the down payment is
below a certain threshold (often 20%), because a smaller down payment
means less valeur nette cushion for the prêteur. It can often be removed once
the borrower's valeur nette in the home reaches a similar threshold, either
through payments or appreciation.

## Exemple pratique

A acheteur puts down 10% instead of 20% on a home. Because their valeur nette
cushion is smaller, the prêteur requires PMI as a condition of approving
the prêt — an added monthly cost until enough valeur nette builds up.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. PMI primarily protects:
   A. The homepropriétaire
   B. The prêteur
   C. The real estate agent
   D. The local government

2. Vrai ou faux: PMI is typically required when the down payment is
   below a certain threshold, often 20%.

3. Can PMI ever be removed from a prêt?
   A. No, it lasts the entire prêt term
   B. Yes, often once enough valeur nette has built up
   C. Only if the borrower refinances with a different prêteur
   D. Only after the prêt is fully paid off

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. PMI protects the prêteur's risk, not the homepropriétaire.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. Smaller down payments (below roughly 20% on many
conventional prêts) often trigger a PMI requirement.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. PMI can often be removed once valeur nette reaches a
sufficient threshold.

## Points clés

- PMI protects the prêteur, not the homepropriétaire.
- It's typically required when the down payment is below a certain
  threshold.
- PMI can often be removed once enough valeur nette has built up.
