# What Is Property Tax, and Who Sets It?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain what property tax generally funds.
- Explain the basic factors that determine a property tax bill.
- Explain why a property tax bill can change without the owner doing
  anything.

## Core Content

### What Property Tax Generally Funds

Property tax is a recurring tax owners pay to local government, based on
the value of their property. It commonly funds local services like
schools, roads, and emergency services, though exact uses vary by
location.

### What Determines the Bill

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: property tax systems vary significantly by
country and even by local jurisdiction; the following describes common
U.S. mechanics.)* A property tax bill is generally calculated using the
property's assessed value multiplied by a local tax rate, set by local
government bodies (like a county or school district), not the property
owner.

### Why the Bill Can Change Without the Owner Doing Anything

If a local government raises its tax rate, or if the property's assessed
value rises due to a local reassessment, the tax bill can increase even
if the owner hasn't changed anything about the property.

## Worked Example

A home assessed at $250,000 in a jurisdiction with a 1.2% tax rate would
owe roughly $3,000 per year in property tax — before any exemptions or
adjustments.

## Practice Quiz

1. Property tax is generally paid to:
   A. The federal government
   B. Local government
   C. The mortgage lender directly, with no local government involvement
   D. The homeowner's insurance company

2. True or False: A homeowner's property tax bill can rise even if they
   haven't made any changes to the property.

3. What two factors generally determine a property tax bill?
   A. The homeowner's income and credit score
   B. The property's assessed value and the local tax rate
   C. The mortgage interest rate and loan term
   D. The size of the down payment

## Answer Key

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Property tax is a local government tax.

### Question 2

Correct answer: True. A rate increase or reassessment can raise the bill
independent of anything the owner does.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Assessed value and local tax rate are the core
inputs.

## Key Takeaways

- Property tax is a recurring local tax based on property value.
- It's calculated from assessed value and a local tax rate.
- The bill can change due to local decisions, not just the owner's
  actions.
