# Qu’est-ce que l’impôt foncier et qui le fixe?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Explain what propriété tax generally funds.
- Explain the basic factors that determine a propriété tax bill.
- Explain why a propriété tax bill can change without the propriétaire doing
  anything.

## Contenu principal

### What Propriété Tax Generally Funds

Propriété tax is a recurring tax propriétaires pay to local government, based on
the valeur of their propriété. It commonly funds local services like
schools, roads, and emergency services, though exact uses vary by
location.

### What Determines the Bill

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: propriété tax systems vary significantly by
country and even by local jurisdiction; the following describes common
U.S. mechanics.)* A propriété tax bill is generally calculated using the
propriété's assessed valeur multiplied by a local tax rate, set by local
government bodies (like a county or school district), not the propriété
propriétaire.

### Why the Bill Can Change Without the Owner Doing Anything

If a local government raises its tax rate, or if the propriété's assessed
valeur rises due to a local reassessment, the tax bill can increase even
if the propriétaire hasn't changed anything about the propriété.

## Exemple pratique

A home assessed at $250,000 in a jurisdiction with a 1.2% tax rate would
owe roughly $3,000 per year in propriété tax — before any exemptions or
adjustments.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Propriété tax is generally paid to:
   A. The federal government
   B. Local government
   C. The hypothèque prêteur directly, with no local government involvement
   D. The homepropriétaire's insurance company

2. Vrai ou faux: A homepropriétaire's propriété tax bill can rise even if they
   haven't made any changes to the propriété.

3. What two factors generally determine a propriété tax bill?
   A. The homepropriétaire's income and credit score
   B. The propriété's assessed valeur and the local tax rate
   C. The hypothèque intérêts rate and prêt term
   D. The size of the down payment

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Propriété tax is a local government tax.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. A rate increase or reassessment can raise the bill
independent of anything the propriétaire does.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Assessed valeur and local tax rate are the core
inputs.

## Points clés

- Propriété tax is a recurring local tax based on propriété valeur.
- It's calculated from assessed valeur and a local tax rate.
- The bill can change due to local decisions, not just the propriétaire's
  actions.
