# Qu’est-ce qu’un privilège sur une propriété?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a privilège in plain terms.
- Name at least two common types of privilèges.
- Explain why a privilège can affect a propriété vente.

## Contenu principal

### What a Lien Is

A privilège is a legal claim against a propriété, usually securing a debt.
Common examples include a hypothèque privilège (the prêteur's claim, covered
earlier), a tax privilège (unpaid propriété taxes), and a mechanic's privilège (an
unpaid contractor's claim for work done on the propriété).

### How a Lien Attaches

A privilège is typically recorded with the local government, attaching to the
propriété itself rather than just to the propriétaire personally — meaning it
can follow the propriété even through a vente if not resolved first.

### Why It Can Block a Sale

Because a privilège is attached to the propriété, most acheteurs and prêteurs will
require it to be cleared before a vente closes. An unresolved privilège is a
common reason a propriété vente gets delayed or falls through.

## Exemple pratique

A homepropriétaire hires a contractor for a renovation and doesn't pay the
final invoice. The contractor can file a mechanic's privilège against the
propriété, which would need to be resolved before the home could be sold
with a clear titre.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A privilège is best described as:
   A. A type of homepropriétaire's insurance
   B. A legal claim against a propriété, usually securing a debt
   C. A government subsidy for homeacheteurs
   D. A required home inspection

2. Vrai ou faux: A privilège only affects the current propriétaire personally, not
   the propriété itself.

3. What is a mechanic's privilège?
   A. A claim filed by an unpaid contractor for work done on the
      propriété
   B. A type of car prêt
   C. A government tax on new construction
   D. A form of titre insurance

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. A

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A privilège is a legal claim against a propriété, usually
tied to a debt.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Liens attach to the propriété itself, which is
why they can follow it through a vente.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: A. A mechanic's privilège is filed by an unpaid contractor
for work performed on the propriété.

## Points clés

- A privilège is a legal claim against a propriété, usually securing a debt.
- Liens attach to the propriété, not just the propriétaire personally.
- Unresolved privilèges commonly block or delay propriété ventes.
