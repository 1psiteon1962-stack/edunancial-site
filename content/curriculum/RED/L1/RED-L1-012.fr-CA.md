# Qu’est-ce qu’une évaluation immobilière et pourquoi diffère-t-elle du prix?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define an évaluation in plain terms.
- Explain who typically orders an évaluation and why.
- Explain why appraised valeur and vente price can be different numbers.

## Contenu principal

### What an Appraisal Is

An évaluation is a professional, independent estimate of a propriété's
valeur, performed by a licensed appraiser. It's based on factors like
comparable ventes, condition, and location.

### Who Orders It, and Why

Lenders typically require an évaluation before approving a hypothèque, to
confirm the propriété is worth roughly what's being borrowed against it —
protecting the prêteur's collateral, not just helping the acheteur.

### Why the Numbers Can Differ

The appraised valeur and the agreed vente price are two separate numbers
determined two separate ways: the vente price is whatever a acheteur and
seller agreed to, while the évaluation is an independent professional
estimate. If a home appraises below the agreed vente price, it can create
a real problem for financing, since the prêteur generally won't prêt more
than the appraised valeur supports.

## Exemple pratique

A acheteur agrees to pay $320,000 for a home, but the évaluation comes back
at $305,000. The prêteur will typically only finance based on the lower
appraised valeur, meaning the acheteur may need a larger down payment or the
vente price may need to be renegotiated.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. An évaluation is:
   A. The price a acheteur and seller agree to
   B. An independent professional estimate of a propriété's valeur
   C. A type of homepropriétaire's insurance
   D. A government tax assessment

2. Vrai ou faux: Lenders typically require an évaluation to protect
   their own collateral, not just to help the acheteur.

3. What can happen if a home appraises below the agreed vente price?
   A. Nothing — the vente proceeds exactly as agreed
   B. The prêteur may only finance up to the appraised valeur, creating a
      financing gap
   C. The évaluation is automatically overturned
   D. Propriété tax is reduced

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. An évaluation is an independent, professional valeur
estimate — separate from the negotiated price.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. The évaluation protects the prêteur's collateral
position on the prêt.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. A low évaluation can create a financing gap that the
acheteur, seller, or both must resolve.

## Points clés

- An évaluation is an independent estimate of valeur, not the vente price.
- Lenders require it to protect their own collateral.
- A low évaluation relative to the vente price can create real financing
  problems.
