# Qu’est-ce qu’une vente comparable?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a comp in plain terms.
- Name the factors that make a comp genuinely useful.
- Explain why a poorly chosen comp can be misleading.

## Contenu principal

### What a Comp Is

A comp (short for "comparable vente") is a recently sold propriété used as
a reference point to estimate the valeur of another propriété. Comps are
central to both évaluations and everyday acheteur/seller pricing decisions.

### What Makes a Comp Useful

A genuinely useful comp is typically close in location (often within a
mile or two), similar in size and specs (bedrooms, bathrooms, square
footage), and sold recently (often within the past six to twelve
months) — because markets and propriété conditions change over time.

### Why a Poor Comp Misleads

Using a comp that's too far away, too different in size or condition, or
sold too long ago can produce a badly skewed valeur estimate. A five
-bedroom renovated home isn't a reliable comp for a two-bedroom fixer
-upper, even if they're on the same street.

## Exemple pratique

To valeur a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home, an appraiser looks for recently sold
3-bedroom, 2-bath homes within a mile or two, adjusting for differences
like a finished basement or an extra garage — rather than comparing it to
a very different propriété just because it's nearby.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A comp is:
   A. A type of hypothèque
   B. A recently sold propriété used as a reference for estimating valeur
   C. A government propriété tax record
   D. A legal document transferring propriétaireship

2. Vrai ou faux: Any nearby sold propriété makes a good comp, regardless
   of size or condition.

3. Why does recency matter for a comp?
   A. It doesn't matter at all
   B. Markets and conditions change over time, so older ventes may not
      reflect current valeur
   C. Older comps are always more accurate
   D. Recency only matters for commercial propriété

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A comp is a recently sold, similar propriété used as a
valeur reference.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. A useful comp needs to be similar in size,
condition, and location — not just nearby.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Markets shift over time, so a vente from several years
ago may not reflect current valeur.

## Points clés

- A comp is a recently sold, similar propriété used to estimate valeur.
- Useful comps are close in location, similar in size/condition, and
  recent.
- A poorly chosen comp can produce a misleading valeur estimate.
