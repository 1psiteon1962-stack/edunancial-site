# Que vérifie réellement une inspection résidentielle?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a home inspection in plain terms.
- Explain how it's different from an évaluation.
- Explain what a acheteur can do with inspection results.

## Contenu principal

### What a Home Inspection Is

A home inspection is a professional evaluation of a propriété's physical
condition — checking systems like the roof, foundation, plumbing,
electrical, and HVAC for existing or potential problems.

### How It's Different from an Appraisal

An évaluation estimates valeur; an inspection assesses condition. A home
can appraise at a high valeur while still having significant physical
problems the évaluation wouldn't necessarily catch in the same detail —
they answer different questions.

### What a Buyer Can Do With the Results

Depending on the purchase agreement, a acheteur who receives a concerning
inspection report may be able to negotiate repairs, request a price
reduction, or in some cases walk away from the deal — the specific
options depend on the contract terms agreed to beforehand.

## Exemple pratique

An inspection reveals an aging roof likely to need replacement within a
few years. The acheteur can use this information to negotiate a price
reduction or ask the seller to address it before closing, depending on
what the purchase agreement allows.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A home inspection primarily evaluates:
   A. The propriété's market valeur
   B. The propriété's physical condition
   C. The acheteur's creditworthiness
   D. The local propriété tax rate

2. Vrai ou faux: An évaluation and a home inspection answer the same
   question.

3. What might a acheteur be able to do after a concerning inspection
   report, depending on the contract?
   A. Nothing at all
   B. Negotiate repairs, a price reduction, or potentially walk away
   C. Automatically cancel the hypothèque
   D. Force the seller to lower the propriété tax

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A home inspection evaluates physical condition, not
market valeur.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. An évaluation estimates valeur; an inspection
assesses condition — different purposes.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Depending on contract terms, a acheteur may have several
options after a concerning inspection.

## Points clés

- A home inspection evaluates physical condition, not valeur.
- It serves a different purpose than an évaluation.
- Inspection results can give a acheteur real negotiating leverage,
  depending on the contract.
