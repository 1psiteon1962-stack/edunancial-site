# Qu’est-ce que l’assurance titres?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define titre assurance in plain terms.
- Explain what it protects against.
- Explain why it's typically a one-time cost.

## Contenu principal

### What Titre Insurance Is

Titre assurance protects the propriétaire (and separately, the prêteur) against
financial loss from titre problems that existed before the purchase but
weren't discovered during the titre search — such as an undisclosed
heir, a forged document in the propriété's history, or an error in public
records.

### What It Protects Against

Unlike most assurance, which covers future events, titre assurance
covers past, undiscovered problems. If a covered issue surfaces after
clôture, the policy can cover legal costs and financial losses tied to
defending or resolving the claim.

### Why It's a One-Time Cost

Because titre assurance covers historical risk rather than ongoing risk,
it's typically paid once, at clôture, rather than as a recurring premium
like homepropriétaire's assurance.

## Exemple pratique

Two years after buying a home, a previously unknown heir of a past propriétaire
files a claim on the propriété. The acheteur's titre assurance policy covers
the legal costs of resolving the claim.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Titre assurance primarily protects against:
   A. Future storm damage
   B. Past, undiscovered titre problems
   C. A drop in market valeur
   D. Late hypothèque payments

2. Vrai ou faux: Titre assurance is typically paid as a recurring
   monthly premium, like homepropriétaire's assurance.

3. Which of these is an example of something titre assurance might
   cover?
   A. A new roof needed after a storm
   B. An undisclosed heir with a claim from the propriété's past
   C. A decline in the local real estate market
   D. A late hypothèque payment fee

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Titre assurance covers past, undiscovered issues —
not future events like storm damage.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. It's typically a one-time payment at clôture.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. An undisclosed heir is a classic example of a
historical titre problem this assurance covers.

## Points clés

- Titre assurance covers past, undiscovered titre problems.
- It's typically a one-time payment made at clôture.
- It protects against financial loss and legal costs if a covered issue
  surfaces later.
