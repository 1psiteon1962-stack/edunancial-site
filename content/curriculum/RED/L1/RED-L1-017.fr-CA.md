# Qu’est-ce qu’un compte en fiducie (escrow)?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define escrow in plain terms.
- Explain the two common uses of the term in real estate.
- Explain why a neutral third party is involved.

## Contenu principal

### Escrow, Generally

Escrow refers to money or documents held by a neutral third party until
specific conditions are met. In real estate, the term is used in two
related but distinct ways.

### Escrow During a Purchase

During a home purchase, an escrow company or agent holds the acheteur's
deposit and relevant documents until all conditions of the sale (like
inspections and financing) are satisfied, then releases funds and
documents to complete the clôture.

### Escrow After Purchase (For Taxes and Insurance)

After clôture, many hypothèque prêteurs maintain an ongoing escrow account,
collecting a portion of propriété impôt and homepropriétaire's assurance costs
with each monthly payment, then paying those bills on the homepropriétaire's
behalf when due.

## Exemple pratique

A acheteur's earnest money deposit is held in escrow during the purchase
process. After clôture, their new prêteur also sets up an ongoing escrow
account to collect and pay propriété impôtes and assurance automatically
each year.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Escrow refers to:
   A. A type of home inspection
   B. Money or documents held by a neutral third party until conditions
      are met
   C. A government propriété impôt
   D. A form of titre assurance

2. Vrai ou faux: Escrow is only used during the home-buying process,
   never afterward.

3. What might an ongoing hypothèque escrow account be used for?
   A. Paying the homepropriétaire's grocery bills
   B. Collecting and paying propriété impôt and assurance
   C. Covering the down payment
   D. Paying the real estate agent's commission

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Escrow is a neutral third-party holding arrangement.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Escrow is also commonly used on an ongoing basis
for impôt and assurance payments after clôture.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Ongoing escrow accounts commonly handle propriété impôt
and assurance payments.

## Points clés

- Escrow is a neutral third-party holding arrangement.
- It's used both during a purchase and, often, on an ongoing basis for
  impôtes and assurance.
- The neutral third party protects both acheteur and vendeur (or prêteur and
  borrower) during the process.
