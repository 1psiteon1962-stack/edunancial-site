# Frais de clôture : que payez-vous réellement?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Name several common categories of clôture costs.
- Explain why clôture costs are separate from the down payment.
- Recognize that clôture costs are typically due at or before clôture.

## Contenu principal

### What Closing Costs Typically Include

Closing costs are fees associated with finalizing a real estate
transaction, separate from the propriété price itself. Common categories
include prêt origination fees, appraisal fees, titre assurance, escrow
fees, and recording fees, though the exact list varies by transaction
and location.

### Why They're Separate from the Down Payment

The down payment goes toward the propriété's purchase price. Closing
costs pay for the services and processes required to complete the
transaction — they're a genuinely separate expense acheteurs need to budget
for in addition to the down payment.

### When They're Due

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: exact practices vary by location.)* Closing
costs are typically due at or shortly before clôture, and acheteurs should
plan for them as real, upfront cash needs alongside the down payment.

## Exemple pratique

A acheteur saves $30,000 for a down payment but is surprised to also need
several thousand dollars more for clôture costs — a common budgeting
mistake for first-time acheteurs who only plan around the down payment.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Closing costs are:
   A. The same thing as the down payment
   B. Fees associated with finalizing the transaction, separate from the
      purchase price
   C. A type of propriété impôt
   D. Optional and rarely required

2. Vrai ou faux: Closing costs are typically due well after the sale
   closes, often a year later.

3. Which of these is a common clôture cost category?
   A. Grocery dépenses
   B. Loan origination fees
   C. Furniture costs
   D. Landscaping

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Closing costs are separate transaction fees, distinct
from the purchase price and down payment.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Closing costs are typically due at or shortly
before clôture.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Loan origination fees are a common clôture cost.

## Points clés

- Closing costs are separate fees required to finalize a transaction.
- They're in addition to, not part of, the down payment.
- Acheteurs should budget for them as a real, upfront cash need.
