# Unifamilial ou multifamilial : quelle est la vraie différence pour un propriétaire?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define single-family and multi-family propriétés.
- Explain how revenu potential differs between the two.
- Explain how management responsibility differs between the two.

## Contenu principal

### What Each Type Is

A single-family propriété houses one household in one independent unit.
A multi-family propriété contains multiple separate units (like a duplex,
triplex, or fourplex) within one propriété, each capable of housing a
different household.

### Income Potential

A single-family home generally generates rental revenu from one source
(if rented at all). A multi-family propriété can generate revenu from
multiple units simultaneously, which can mean more total revenu — but
also depends on more units being successfully rented and maintained.

### Management Responsibility

More units generally mean more day-to-day management: more tenants, more
potential maintenance requests, more turnover events. This trade-off
between revenu potential and management complexity is a real
consideration when comparing propriété types, not just a difference in
price.

## Exemple pratique

A single-family rental generates one rent check per month. A fourplex
generates up to four rent checks per month from the same overall
propriété — but also up to four tenants' worth of maintenance requests
and turnover to manage.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A multi-family propriété is best described as:
   A. A propriété with one independent housing unit
   B. A propriété containing multiple separate housing units
   C. A propriété with no housing units
   D. A type of commercial office building

2. Vrai ou faux: Multi-family propriétés generally involve more
   day-to-day management than single-family propriétés.

3. What is one real trade-off between single-family and multi-family
   propriétaireship?
   A. There is no trade-off; multi-family is always better
   B. Multi-family offers more revenu potential but generally more
      management complexity
   C. Single-family always generates more total revenu
   D. Multi-family propriétés cannot be rented out

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Multi-family propriétés contain multiple separate
housing units.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. More units generally mean more tenants and more
management responsibility.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. More revenu potential typically comes with more
management complexity.

## Points clés

- Single-family propriétés house one unit; multi-family propriétés
  house multiple units.
- Multi-family propriétés offer more revenu potential from multiple
  units.
- That revenu potential comes with real trade-offs in management
  complexity.
