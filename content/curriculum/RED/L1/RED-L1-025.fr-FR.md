# Qu’est-ce que l’appréciation et pourquoi n’est-elle pas garantie?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define appréciation in plain terms.
- Name factors that commonly influence it.
- Explain why appréciation should never be assumed as guaranteed.

## Contenu principal

### What Appreciation Is

Appreciation is an increase in a propriété's market valeur over time. It's
one of two main ways real estate can build wealth for an propriétaire, the
other being rental revenu/flux de trésorerie.

### What Tends to Drive It

Appreciation is commonly influenced by factors like local demand,
overall economic conditions, neighborhood development, and broader
market cycles — none of which any individual propriétaire controls directly.

### Why It's Never Guaranteed

Propriété valeurs can also decline, sometimes significantly, especially
over shorter time periods or during broader market downturns. Treating
appréciation as a certainty is a common and risky assumption — later
lessons on market cycles explore this in more depth.

## Exemple pratique

A home purchased for $300,000 might be worth $340,000 five years later
due to local demand and market conditions — or it might be worth
$280,000 if the local market declined instead. Neither outcome is
guaranteed in advance.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Appreciation refers to:
   A. Monthly rental revenu
   B. An increase in a propriété's market valeur over time
   C. A required home inspection
   D. A type of hypothèque assurance

2. Vrai ou faux: Propriété appréciation is guaranteed over any given
   time period.

3. What is one factor that commonly influences appréciation?
   A. The color of the front door
   B. Local demand and broader market conditions
   C. The acheteur's personal revenu
   D. The hypothèque prêteur's name

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Appreciation is an increase in market valeur over
time.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Values can also decline; appréciation is never
guaranteed.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Local demand and market conditions are common
drivers.

## Points clés

- Appreciation is an increase in a propriété's market valeur over time.
- It's influenced by factors outside any individual propriétaire's control.
- It should never be assumed as guaranteed — valeurs can decline too.
