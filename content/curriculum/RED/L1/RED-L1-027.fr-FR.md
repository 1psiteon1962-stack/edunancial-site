# What Is a 1031 Exchange? (What It Is, in Plain Terms)

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Explain the basic idea behind a 1031 exchange.
- Explain what "deferring" impôt means, as opposed to eliminating it.
- Recognize that strict rules and timelines apply.

## Contenu principal

### The Basic Idea

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: this is a U.S. impôt provision; not available in
other countries.)* A 1031 exchange allows an investor to sell one
investissement propriété and reinvest the proceeds into another "like-kind"
propriété, deferring the capital gains impôt that would normally be due on
the sale.

### Deferring, Not Eliminating

This is a critical distinction: the impôt isn't erased, it's postponed.
The deferred gain generally carries forward into the new propriété, and
impôt may eventually be owed later, depending on what happens with future
transactions.

### Strict Rules Apply

*ASSUMPTION (for teaching purposes):* 1031 exchanges involve specific
identification and closing timelines and procedural requirements. This
introductory lesson doesn't cover those specifics — the goal here is
understanding the core concept before the mechanics.

## Exemple pratique

An investor sells a rental propriété with a significant gain and, instead
of paying capital gains impôt immediately, uses a 1031 exchange to roll
the proceeds into a new rental propriété — deferring that impôt bill rather
than paying it right away.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A 1031 exchange primarily allows an investor to:
   A. Eliminate capital gains impôt permanently
   B. Defer capital gains impôt by reinvesting in a like-kind propriété
   C. Avoid propriété impôt entirely
   D. Skip the need for a home inspection

2. Vrai ou faux: A 1031 exchange eliminates the impôt owed rather than
   postponing it.

3. What does "like-kind" generally refer to in this context?
   A. Two identical houses
   B. Investment properties that qualify for exchange treatment under
      the rules
   C. Any two assets of equal price
   D. A specific brand of propriété

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A 1031 exchange defers, not eliminates, capital gains
impôt.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. The impôt is postponed, not erased.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. "Like-kind" refers to qualifying investissement propriété
types under the applicable rules, not identical properties.

## Points clés

- A 1031 exchange defers capital gains impôt by reinvesting proceeds into
  another qualifying propriété.
- Deferring is not the same as eliminating the impôt.
- Strict rules and timelines apply, covered in more depth in later
  lessons.
