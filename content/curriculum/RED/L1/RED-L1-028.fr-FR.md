# Propriétaire-bailleur Basics: What Are You Actually Responsible For?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Name core landlord responsibilities.
- Distinguish landlord responsibilities from locataire responsibilities.
- Explain why understanding this split matters before renting out a
  propriété.

## Contenu principal

### Core Propriétaire-bailleur Responsibilities

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: exact obligations vary by state/country and
bail terms.)* Propriétaire-bailleurs are generally responsible for maintaining the
propriété in a habitable condition — this typically includes major
systems (plumbing, heating, structural issues) and compliance with local
housing codes.

### What Tenants Are Typically Responsible For

Tenants are generally responsible for day-to-day upkeep of their living
space, following bail terms, and often reporting problems promptly —
the specific split is usually spelled out in the bail itself.

### Why the Split Matters

A new landlord who doesn't understand these responsibilities risks legal
exposure (for failing habitability obligations) or unnecessary costs (for
covering things that were actually the locataire's responsibility). Clear
bail terms help prevent disputes on both sides.

## Exemple pratique

A locataire reports a broken heater in winter. This is typically a landlord
responsibility (a major system affecting habitability), not something the
locataire is expected to fix themselves.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Propriétaire-bailleurs are generally responsible for:
   A. Day-to-day tidiness of the locataire's space
   B. Maintaining the propriété in a habitable condition, including major
      systems
   C. The locataire's personal belongings
   D. The locataire's employment status

2. Vrai ou faux: The exact split of landlord and locataire responsibilities
   is identical everywhere, regardless of location or bail.

3. Why does understanding this responsibility split matter for a new
   landlord?
   A. It doesn't matter at all
   B. It helps avoid legal exposure and unnecessary costs
   C. It only matters for commercial propriété
   D. It has no connection to the bail

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Propriétaire-bailleurs generally handle habitability and major
systems.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Responsibilities vary by location and bail
terms.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Understanding the split helps avoid legal and
financial problems.

## Points clés

- Propriétaire-bailleurs generally handle habitability and major systems.
- Tenants generally handle day-to-day upkeep and following bail terms.
- Clear bail terms help prevent disputes over responsibility.
