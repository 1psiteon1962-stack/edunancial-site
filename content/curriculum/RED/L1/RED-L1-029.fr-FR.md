# What Is a Lease, and What Should Be In One?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a bail in plain terms.
- Name core terms a bail should address.
- Explain why a verbal agreement is a real risk.

## Contenu principal

### What a Lease Is

A bail is a legally binding contract between a landlord and locataire,
setting the terms under which the locataire may occupy the propriété.

### Core Terms a Lease Should Address

A solid bail typically addresses: rent amount and due date, bail term
length, security deposit terms, maintenance responsibilities, and rules
around things like pets or subletting. Vague or missing terms are a
common source of disputes.

### Why "We'll Figure It Out" Is a Real Risk

A verbal or informal agreement, without clear written terms, leaves both
landlord and locataire exposed if a disagreement arises later — there's no
clear record of what was actually agreed to. A written bail protects
both parties, not just the landlord.

## Exemple pratique

A landlord and locataire verbally agree on rent but never document a move
-out notice requirement. When the locataire leaves without notice, the
landlord has no written term to point to, creating a real dispute that a
clear bail could have prevented.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A bail is:
   A. An informal handshake agreement
   B. A legally binding contract setting occupancy terms
   C. A type of propriété impôt
   D. A government housing subsidy

2. Vrai ou faux: A verbal bail agreement carries the same protection
   as a clear written bail.

3. Which of these is a core term a bail should typically address?
   A. The locataire's favorite color
   B. Rent amount and due date
   C. The landlord's personal hobbies
   D. The weather forecast

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A bail is a legally binding contract.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. A written bail provides much clearer protection
than a verbal agreement.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Rent amount and due date are core bail terms.

## Points clés

- A bail is a legally binding contract setting occupancy terms.
- Core terms include rent, term length, deposits, and responsibilities.
- A written bail protects both landlord and locataire far better than a
  verbal agreement.
