# What Is Expulsion, and Why Is It a Last Resort, Not a First Step?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define eviction in plain terms.
- Explain why it requires a formal legal process.
- Explain why it's generally treated as a last resort.

## Contenu principal

### What Expulsion Is

Expulsion is the formal legal process of removing a locataire from a rental
propriété, typically due to bail violations like unpaid rent.

### Why It Requires a Formal Process

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: exact procedures vary significantly by
location.)* A landlord generally cannot simply remove a locataire or their
belongings without going through the legal eviction process — doing so
("self-help eviction") can expose the landlord to serious legal
liability, even if the locataire is genuinely behind on rent.

### Why It's a Last Resort

Expulsion is time-consuming, costly, and disruptive for both parties. Most
experienced landlords treat it as a final step after other options
(communication, payment plans, formal notices) have been exhausted, not
a first response to a missed payment.

## Exemple pratique

A locataire misses one rent payment. Rather than immediately filing for
eviction, an experienced landlord might first send a formal notice and
attempt to resolve the issue directly, reserving eviction for situations
that remain unresolved.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Expulsion is:
   A. An informal request to leave
   B. A formal legal process for removing a locataire
   C. A type of bail renewal
   D. A landlord's personal decision requiring no legal process

2. Vrai ou faux: A landlord can legally remove a locataire's belongings
   without going through the formal eviction process.

3. Why do many landlords treat eviction as a last resort?
   A. It's quick and inexpensive
   B. It's time-consuming, costly, and disruptive for both parties
   C. It's required after every missed payment
   D. It has no legal requirements

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Expulsion is a formal legal process.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Self-help eviction (bypassing the legal process)
can expose a landlord to real liability.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. The cost, time, and disruption make it a last resort
for most landlords.

## Points clés

- Expulsion is a formal legal process, not an informal removal.
- Bypassing that process can create real legal liability for a landlord.
- Most landlords treat eviction as a last resort after other options are
  exhausted.
