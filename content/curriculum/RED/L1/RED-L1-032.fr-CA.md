# What Is a Gestionnaire immobilier, and When Do You Need One?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define what a propriété manager does.
- Explain the basic cost trade-off of hiring one.
- Identify a situation where hiring one might make sense.

## Contenu principal

### What a Gestionnaire immobilier Does

A propriété manager handles day-to-day landlord responsibilities on an
propriétaire's behalf — things like collecting rent, coordinating repairs,
screening locataires, and handling locataire communication.

### The Basic Cost Trade-Off

Propriété managers typically charge a percentage of collected rent (often
in a broad range depending on market and services) as a fee. This is a
genuine trade-off: less hands-on time and effort for the propriétaire, in
exchange for reduced net revenu.

### When It Starts to Make Sense

Owners with multiple properties, properties far from where they live, or
limited time/interest in hands-on management often find a propriété
manager's fee worthwhile. A single, nearby rental that an propriétaire enjoys
managing personally may not need one at all — this is a genuine
individual decision, not a universal rule.

## Exemple pratique

An investor who owns rental properties in three different cities, far
from where they live, hires local propriété managers in each location
rather than trying to personally handle locataire issues and repairs
remotely.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A propriété manager typically:
   A. Owns the propriété alongside the investor
   B. Handles day-to-day landlord responsibilities on the propriétaire's behalf
   C. Provides the down payment for a purchase
   D. Sets local propriété impôt rates

2. Vrai ou faux: Propriété managers typically work for free, with no
   fee involved.

3. What is one situation where hiring a propriété manager often makes
   sense?
   A. Owning one nearby propriété the propriétaire enjoys managing
   B. Owning multiple properties far from where the propriétaire lives
   C. Never owning any rental propriété
   D. Living directly next door to a single rental

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A propriété manager handles day-to-day responsibilities
for the propriétaire.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Propriété managers typically charge a fee, often a
percentage of collected rent.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Distance and multiple properties often make a
propriété manager worthwhile.

## Points clés

- A propriété manager handles day-to-day landlord tasks for a fee.
- The trade-off is reduced net revenu for less hands-on effort.
- Whether it makes sense depends on the propriétaire's situation, not a
  universal rule.
