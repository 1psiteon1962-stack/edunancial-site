# Residential vs. Commercial Real Estate: What Actually Changes?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define residential and commercial real estate.
- Name at least one way financing differs between the two.
- Name at least one way valuation differs between the two.

## Core Content

### What Each Category Generally Means

Residential real estate is generally intended for people to live in.
Commercial real estate is generally intended for business use — office
buildings, retail spaces, and larger multi-family properties (often 5+
units, covered in more depth in Level 2) are commonly classified as
commercial.

### How Financing Differs

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: specifics vary by lender and location.)*
Commercial financing is generally underwritten differently than
residential financing — often requiring larger down payments and shorter
loan terms, with more emphasis on the property's income-generating
ability.

### How Valuation Differs

Residential properties are commonly valued using comps (comparable
sales). Commercial properties are often valued more heavily based on
income potential (like cap rate, introduced earlier in this track) —
reflecting their different underlying purpose.

## Worked Example

A single-family home's value is estimated by comparing it to similar
nearby homes that recently sold. An office building's value is more
likely estimated based on its rental income potential relative to its
price.

## Practice Quiz

1. Commercial real estate is generally intended for:
   A. Personal living space only
   B. Business use
   C. Government housing only
   D. Agricultural use exclusively

2. True or False: Residential and commercial properties are typically
   financed and valued in exactly the same way.

3. Residential properties are commonly valued using:
   A. Cap rate exclusively
   B. Comparable sales (comps)
   C. Zoning classification alone
   D. The owner's personal income

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Commercial real estate is generally for business use.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Financing and valuation approaches commonly
differ between the two categories.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Comps are the common residential valuation approach.

## Key Takeaways

- Residential real estate is for living; commercial is for business use.
- Financing approaches commonly differ between the two.
- Valuation methods commonly differ too — comps for residential, income
  potential for commercial.
