# Résidentiel vs. Commercial Immobilier: What Actually Changes?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define residential and commercial real estate.
- Name at least one way financing differs between the two.
- Name at least one way valuation differs between the two.

## Contenu principal

### What Each Category Generally Means

Résidentiel real estate is generally intended for people to live in.
Commercial real estate is generally intended for business use — office
buildings, retail spaces, and larger multi-family properties (often 5+
units, covered in more depth in Level 2) are commonly classified as
commercial.

### How Financing Differs

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: specifics vary by lender and location.)*
Commercial financing is generally underwritten differently than
residential financing — often requiring larger down payments and shorter
loan terms, with more emphasis on the propriété's revenu-generating
ability.

### How Valuation Differs

Résidentiel properties are commonly valeurd using comps (comparable
sales). Commercial properties are often valeurd more heavily based on
revenu potential (like cap rate, introduced earlier in this track) —
reflecting their different underlying purpose.

## Exemple pratique

A single-family home's valeur is estimated by comparing it to similar
nearby homes that recently sold. An office building's valeur is more
likely estimated based on its rental revenu potential relative to its
price.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Commercial real estate is generally intended for:
   A. Personal living space only
   B. Business use
   C. Government housing only
   D. Agricultural use exclusively

2. Vrai ou faux: Résidentiel and commercial properties are typically
   financed and valeurd in exactly the same way.

3. Résidentiel properties are commonly valeurd using:
   A. Cap rate exclusively
   B. Comparable sales (comps)
   C. Zonage classification alone
   D. The propriétaire's personal revenu

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Commercial real estate is generally for business use.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Financing and valuation approaches commonly
differ between the two categories.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Comps are the common residential valuation approach.

## Points clés

- Résidentiel real estate is for living; commercial is for business use.
- Financing approaches commonly differ between the two.
- Valuation methods commonly differ too — comps for residential, revenu
  potential for commercial.
