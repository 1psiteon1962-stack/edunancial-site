# What Is a REIT? (Real Estate Without Owning Property Directly)

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define a REIT in plain terms.
- Explain how it differs from owning physical property directly.
- Identify one trade-off of investing through a REIT.

## Core Content

### What a REIT Is

A REIT (Real Estate Investment Trust) is a company that owns and
typically operates income-producing real estate, with shares that trade
similarly to a stock. Buying REIT shares gives an investor exposure to
real estate returns without buying or managing physical property
directly.

### How It Differs from Direct Ownership

A REIT investor doesn't personally handle tenants, repairs, or property
management — that's handled by the REIT itself. This connects back to
the WHITE-box paper asset concept: a REIT share is a paper asset offering
real-estate-linked returns, blending the RED and WHITE boxes.

### A Real Trade-Off

In exchange for that hands-off convenience, a REIT investor gives up
direct control over which specific properties are bought, sold, or
managed, and how — decisions made by the REIT's management instead of
the individual investor.

## Worked Example

An investor who wants real estate exposure but doesn't want to manage
tenants or repairs buys shares of a REIT instead of purchasing a rental
property directly — trading direct control for convenience.

## Practice Quiz

1. A REIT is:
   A. A type of mortgage
   B. A company owning income-producing real estate, with tradable
      shares
   C. A government housing program
   D. A required home inspection

2. True or False: A REIT investor personally manages the properties the
   REIT owns.

3. What is one trade-off of investing through a REIT instead of buying
   property directly?
   A. No trade-off exists
   B. The investor gives up direct control over specific property
      decisions
   C. REITs guarantee higher returns than direct ownership
   D. REITs eliminate all investment risk

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. A REIT is a company owning real estate, with
tradable shares.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. The REIT's management handles property decisions,
not individual shareholders.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Investors trade direct control for hands-off
convenience.

## Key Takeaways

- A REIT offers real estate exposure through tradable shares.
- It blends the RED and WHITE boxes — a paper asset linked to real
  estate.
- Investors give up direct property control in exchange for convenience.
