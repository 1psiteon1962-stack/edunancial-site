# What Is a REIT? (Immobilier Without Owning Propriété Directly)

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a REIT in plain terms.
- Explain how it differs from owning physical propriété directly.
- Identify one trade-off of investing through a REIT.

## Contenu principal

### What a REIT Is

A REIT (Immobilier Investment Trust) is a company that owns and
typically operates revenu-producing real estate, with shares that trade
similarly to a stock. Buying REIT shares gives an investor exposure to
real estate returns without buying or managing physical propriété
directly.

### How It Differs from Direct Ownership

A REIT investor doesn't personally handle locataires, repairs, or propriété
management — that's handled by the REIT itself. This connects back to
the WHITE-box paper asset concept: a REIT share is a paper asset offering
real-estate-linked returns, blending the RED and WHITE boxes.

### A Real Trade-Off

In exchange for that hands-off convenience, a REIT investor gives up
direct control over which specific properties are bought, sold, or
managed, and how — decisions made by the REIT's management instead of
the individual investor.

## Exemple pratique

An investor who wants real estate exposure but doesn't want to manage
locataires or repairs buys shares of a REIT instead of purchasing a rental
propriété directly — trading direct control for convenience.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A REIT is:
   A. A type of hypothèque
   B. A company owning revenu-producing real estate, with tradable
      shares
   C. A government housing program
   D. A required home inspection

2. Vrai ou faux: A REIT investor personally manages the properties the
   REIT owns.

3. What is one trade-off of investing through a REIT instead of buying
   propriété directly?
   A. No trade-off exists
   B. The investor gives up direct control over specific propriété
      decisions
   C. REITs guarantee higher returns than direct propriétaireship
   D. REITs eliminate all investissement risk

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A REIT is a company owning real estate, with
tradable shares.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. The REIT's management handles propriété decisions,
not individual shareholders.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Investors trade direct control for hands-off
convenience.

## Points clés

- A REIT offers real estate exposure through tradable shares.
- It blends the RED and WHITE boxes — a paper asset linked to real
  estate.
- Investors give up direct propriété control in exchange for convenience.
