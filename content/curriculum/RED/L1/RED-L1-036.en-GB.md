# What Is House Hacking?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define house hacking in plain terms.
- Explain how it can reduce an owner's housing costs.
- Identify a real limitation of the strategy.

## Core Content

### What House Hacking Is

House hacking means living in part of a property while renting out
another part — commonly done by buying a small multi-family property
(like a duplex or triplex), living in one unit, and renting the others.

### How It Reduces Housing Costs

Rental income from the other units can offset some or all of the owner's
own mortgage payment, effectively reducing (or in some cases eliminating)
their personal housing cost while they build equity.

### A Real Limitation

House hacking requires living close to tenants, which isn't appealing or
practical for everyone, and it still involves real landlord
responsibilities (covered earlier in this track) even though the owner
also lives on-site.

## Worked Example

An owner buys a duplex, lives in one unit, and rents the other for
$1,400/month. If their total mortgage payment is $1,800/month, their
actual out-of-pocket housing cost drops to $400/month.

## Practice Quiz

1. House hacking generally means:
   A. Renting out an entire property while living elsewhere
   B. Living in part of a property while renting out another part
   C. Flipping properties for quick profit
   D. Avoiding property taxes entirely

2. True or False: House hacking can reduce an owner's effective housing
   cost through rental income.

3. What is one real limitation of house hacking?
   A. It's illegal in most places
   B. It requires living close to tenants and still involves landlord
      responsibilities
   C. It guarantees the owner will never pay a mortgage
   D. It only works for commercial properties

## Answer Key

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. House hacking means living in part of a property
while renting out the rest.

### Question 2

Correct answer: True. Rental income can offset the owner's own housing
cost.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Proximity to tenants and ongoing landlord
responsibilities are real limitations.

## Key Takeaways

- House hacking means living in part of a property while renting the
  rest.
- It can meaningfully reduce an owner's effective housing cost.
- It still involves real landlord responsibilities and proximity to
  tenants.
