# Qu’est-ce que le house hacking?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define house hacking in plain terms.
- Explain how it can reduce an propriétaire's housing costs.
- Identify a real limitation of the strategy.

## Contenu principal

### What House Hacking Is

House hacking means living in part of a propriété while renting out
another part — commonly done by buying a small multi-family propriété
(like a duplex or triplex), living in one unit, and renting the others.

### How It Reduces Housing Costs

Rental revenu from the other units can offset some or all of the propriétaire's
own hypothèque payment, effectively reducing (or in some cases eliminating)
their personal housing cost while they build equity.

### A Real Limitation

House hacking requires living close to locataires, which isn't appealing or
practical for everyone, and it still involves real landlord
responsibilities (covered earlier in this track) even though the propriétaire
also lives on-site.

## Exemple pratique

An propriétaire buys a duplex, lives in one unit, and rents the other for
$1,400/month. If their total hypothèque payment is $1,800/month, their
actual out-of-pocket housing cost drops to $400/month.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. House hacking generally means:
   A. Renting out an entire propriété while living elsewhere
   B. Living in part of a propriété while renting out another part
   C. Flipping properties for quick profit
   D. Avoiding propriété taxes entirely

2. Vrai ou faux: House hacking can reduce an propriétaire's effective housing
   cost through locatif revenu.

3. What is one real limitation of house hacking?
   A. It's illegal in most places
   B. It requires living close to locataires and still involves landlord
      responsibilities
   C. It guarantees the propriétaire will never pay a hypothèque
   D. It only works for commercial properties

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. House hacking means living in part of a propriété
while renting out the rest.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. Rental revenu can offset the propriétaire's own housing
cost.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Proximity to locataires and ongoing landlord
responsibilities are real limitations.

## Points clés

- House hacking means living in part of a propriété while renting the
  rest.
- It can meaningfully reduce an propriétaire's effective housing cost.
- It still involves real landlord responsibilities and proximity to
  locataires.
