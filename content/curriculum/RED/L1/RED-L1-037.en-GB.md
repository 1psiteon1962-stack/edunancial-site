# What Is Wholesaling in Real Estate?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define wholesaling in plain terms.
- Explain how it differs from actually buying a property.
- Explain why the strategy depends on finding motivated sellers.

## Core Content

### What Wholesaling Is

Wholesaling involves getting a property under contract at a favorable
price, then assigning (selling) that contract to another buyer for a
fee — often without the wholesaler ever actually owning the property.

### How It Differs from Buying Property

A traditional investor buys and holds or renovates a property.
A wholesaler generally never takes ownership at all — the profit comes
from the difference between the contract price and what the end buyer
pays for the contract, covered in more detail in a later lesson on
assignment of contract.

### Why It Depends on Motivated Sellers

Wholesaling generally only works when a seller is willing to sell below
typical market value, often due to urgency (financial distress, an
inherited property, or a need to sell quickly). Finding these sellers
consistently is the core skill the strategy depends on.

## Worked Example

A wholesaler finds a distressed property, gets it under contract for
$150,000, then assigns that contract to an investor willing to pay
$160,000 to take it over — the wholesaler profits $10,000 without ever
owning the property.

## Practice Quiz

1. Wholesaling in real estate generally involves:
   A. Buying and renovating a property personally
   B. Getting a property under contract and assigning that contract to
      another buyer for a fee
   C. Renting out a property long-term
   D. Managing property on behalf of another owner

2. True or False: A wholesaler typically takes full ownership of the
   property before reselling it.

3. What does wholesaling generally depend on finding?
   A. Buyers with excellent credit only
   B. Motivated sellers willing to sell below typical market value
   C. Properties with no legal issues at all
   D. Government subsidies

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Wholesaling involves assigning a purchase contract
for a fee.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Wholesalers typically never take ownership at
all.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Motivated sellers willing to sell below market value
are central to the strategy.

## Key Takeaways

- Wholesaling means assigning a purchase contract for a fee, not buying
  the property.
- The wholesaler typically never takes ownership.
- The strategy depends heavily on finding motivated sellers.
