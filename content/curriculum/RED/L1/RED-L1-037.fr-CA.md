# What Is Wholesaling in Immobilier?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define wholesaling in plain terms.
- Explain how it differs from actually buying a propriété.
- Explain why the strategy depends on finding motivated sellers.

## Contenu principal

### What Wholesaling Is

Wholesaling involves getting a propriété under contract at a favorable
price, then assigning (selling) that contract to another buyer for a
fee — often without the wholesaler ever actually owning the propriété.

### How It Differs from Buying Propriété

A traditional investor buys and holds or renovates a propriété.
A wholesaler generally never takes propriétaireship at all — the profit comes
from the difference between the contract price and what the end buyer
pays for the contract, covered in more detail in a later lesson on
assignment of contract.

### Why It Depends on Motivated Sellers

Wholesaling generally only works when a seller is willing to sell below
typical market valeur, often due to urgency (financial distress, an
inherited propriété, or a need to sell quickly). Finding these sellers
consistently is the core skill the strategy depends on.

## Exemple pratique

A wholesaler finds a distressed propriété, gets it under contract for
$150,000, then assigns that contract to an investor willing to pay
$160,000 to take it over — the wholesaler profits $10,000 without ever
owning the propriété.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Wholesaling in real estate generally involves:
   A. Buying and renovating a propriété personally
   B. Getting a propriété under contract and assigning that contract to
      another buyer for a fee
   C. Renting out a propriété long-term
   D. Managing propriété on behalf of another propriétaire

2. Vrai ou faux: A wholesaler typically takes full propriétaireship of the
   propriété before reselling it.

3. What does wholesaling generally depend on finding?
   A. Buyers with excellent credit only
   B. Motivated sellers willing to sell below typical market valeur
   C. Properties with no legal issues at all
   D. Government subsidies

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Wholesaling involves assigning a purchase contract
for a fee.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Wholesalers typically never take propriétaireship at
all.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Motivated sellers willing to sell below market valeur
are central to the strategy.

## Points clés

- Wholesaling means assigning a purchase contract for a fee, not buying
  the propriété.
- The wholesaler typically never takes propriétaireship.
- The strategy depends heavily on finding motivated sellers.
