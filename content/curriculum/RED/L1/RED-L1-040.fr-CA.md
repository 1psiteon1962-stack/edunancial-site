# Qu’est-ce qu’une cotisation spéciale d’une association de propriétaires?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a HOA special assessment in plain terms.
- Explain why one might occur.
- Recognize it as a real, unplanned cost risk of HOA propriétaireship.

## Contenu principal

### What a Special Assessment Is

A special assessment is an additional, one-time fee a HOA charges its
members, beyond regular dues, usually to cover an unexpected or large
expense the HOA's regular budget doesn't cover.

### Why One Might Occur

Common triggers include major unexpected repairs (like a roof or a
shared structure), insufficient reserve funds for a planned project, or
damage from an event like a storm. If the HOA's ongoing dues and reserve
fund aren't enough, a special assessment fills the gap.

### Why It's a Real Risk to Be Aware Of

Because a special assessment can be a significant, unplanned expense, a
buyer considering a HOA propriété should review the HOA's financial
health and reserve fund status beforehand — a healthy reserve fund
reduces (though doesn't eliminate) the risk of a large surprise
assessment.

## Exemple pratique

A condo building's roof needs replacement, and the HOA's reserve fund
only covers part of the cost. The HOA issues a special assessment,
requiring each unit propriétaire to pay several thousand dollars beyond their
normal monthly dues.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A HOA special assessment is:
   A. A regular monthly HOA fee
   B. An additional, one-time fee to cover an unexpected or large
      expense
   C. A government propriété tax
   D. A fee charged only to new propriétaires

2. Vrai ou faux: A well-funded HOA reserve fund can reduce the risk of a
   large special assessment.

3. What might trigger a special assessment?
   A. A routine, fully budgeted expense
   B. A major unexpected repair not covered by regular dues and reserves
   C. A rise in the propriétaire's personal revenu
   D. A change in hypothèque interest rates

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A special assessment is a one-time fee for an
unexpected or large expense.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. A healthy reserve fund reduces (but doesn't
eliminate) this risk.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Major unexpected repairs are a common trigger.

## Points clés

- A special assessment is a one-time fee beyond regular HOA dues.
- It's typically triggered by unexpected or underfunded major expenses.
- Reviewing a HOA's financial health before buying can reduce this risk.
