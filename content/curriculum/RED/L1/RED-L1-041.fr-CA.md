# Que couvre réellement l’assurance habitation — et que ne couvre-t-elle pas?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Name common categories a standard homepropriétaire's policy covers.
- Identify a common gap in standard coverage.
- Explain why reading the policy details matters.

## Contenu principal

### What It Typically Covers

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: exact coverage varies by policy and location.)*
A standard homepropriétaire's assurance policy commonly covers the structure
itself, personal belongings, liability for injuries on the propriété, and
additional living expenses if the home becomes temporarily uninhabitable
due to a covered event.

### A Common Gap

Standard policies commonly exclude certain events, like flooding — which
often requires a separate policy entirely, covered in a later lesson.
Assuming "homepropriétaire's assurance covers everything" is a common and
costly misunderstanding.

### Why Reading the Details Matters

Coverage limits, exclusions, and deductibles vary significantly between
policies and insurers. A policy that looks similar on the surface can
have very different real protection depending on its specific terms.

## Exemple pratique

A homepropriétaire assumes their standard policy covers flood damage. When a
flood occurs, they discover flooding is excluded from their standard
policy and would have required separate flood assurance to be covered.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A standard homepropriétaire's policy commonly covers:
   A. Nothing related to the structure itself
   B. The structure, belongings, liability, and additional living
      expenses
   C. Only the land, not the structure
   D. Flood damage automatically in every case

2. Vrai ou faux: Standard homepropriétaire's assurance automatically includes
   flood coverage.

3. Why does reading policy details matter?
   A. It doesn't — all policies are identical
   B. Coverage limits and exclusions vary significantly between policies
   C. Only the price matters, not the coverage
   D. Policies never have exclusions

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Standard coverage commonly includes these core
categories.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. Flood coverage is a common standard exclusion.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Coverage varies meaningfully between policies and
insurers.

## Points clés

- Standard homepropriétaire's assurance covers structure, belongings,
  liability, and living expenses.
- Flooding is a common exclusion requiring separate coverage.
- Reading policy details matters because coverage varies significantly.
