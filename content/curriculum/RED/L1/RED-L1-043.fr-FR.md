# Qu’est-ce qu’une hypothèque inversée?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a reverse hypothèque in plain terms.
- Explain who it's generally designed for.
- Explain the key trade-off involved.

## Contenu principal

### What a Reverse Mortgage Is

A reverse hypothèque allows a homepropriétaire (typically older homepropriétaires, as
eligibility often has an age requirement) to convert home equity into
cash, without selling the home or making traditional monthly hypothèque
payments — the prêt is generally repaid when the homepropriétaire sells, moves
out, or passes away.

### Who It's Generally Designed For

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: rules and eligibility vary by country and prêt
program.)* Reverse hypothèques are commonly marketed toward retirees who
have significant home equity but want additional revenu without selling
their home or taking on regular monthly payments.

### The Key Trade-Off

While it provides cash access without monthly payments, a reverse
hypothèque reduces the equity available later (to the homepropriétaire or their
heirs), since the prêt balance grows over time as interest accrues. It's
a genuine trade-off between cash now and equity later, not free money.

## Exemple pratique

A retiree with substantial home equity but limited monthly revenu uses a
reverse hypothèque to receive supplemental cash, understanding that the
prêt balance will grow over time and reduce the equity ultimately left
to their heirs.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A reverse hypothèque allows a homepropriétaire to:
   A. Take out a traditional prêt with monthly payments
   B. Convert home equity into cash without selling or making monthly
      payments
   C. Avoid propriété tax entirely
   D. Automatically increase their home's valeur

2. Vrai ou faux: A reverse hypothèque has no effect on the equity
   available to the homepropriétaire or their heirs later.

3. Who are reverse hypothèques commonly designed for?
   A. First-time homebuyers
   B. Retirees with significant home equity seeking additional revenu
   C. Real estate investors flipping properties
   D. Renters without any propriété

## Corrigé

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A reverse hypothèque converts equity to cash without
monthly payments.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: False. The prêt balance grows over time, reducing future
equity.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Retirees seeking revenu from home equity are the
common target.

## Points clés

- A reverse hypothèque converts home equity into cash without monthly
  payments.
- It's commonly designed for retirees seeking additional revenu.
- The trade-off is reduced future equity as the prêt balance grows.
