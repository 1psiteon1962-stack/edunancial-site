# What Is a Reverse Mortgage?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define a reverse mortgage in plain terms.
- Explain who it's generally designed for.
- Explain the key trade-off involved.

## Core Content

### What a Reverse Mortgage Is

A reverse mortgage allows a homeowner (typically older homeowners, as
eligibility often has an age requirement) to convert home equity into
cash, without selling the home or making traditional monthly mortgage
payments — the loan is generally repaid when the homeowner sells, moves
out, or passes away.

### Who It's Generally Designed For

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: rules and eligibility vary by country and loan
program.)* Reverse mortgages are commonly marketed toward retirees who
have significant home equity but want additional income without selling
their home or taking on regular monthly payments.

### The Key Trade-Off

While it provides cash access without monthly payments, a reverse
mortgage reduces the equity available later (to the homeowner or their
heirs), since the loan balance grows over time as interest accrues. It's
a genuine trade-off between cash now and equity later, not free money.

## Worked Example

A retiree with substantial home equity but limited monthly income uses a
reverse mortgage to receive supplemental cash, understanding that the
loan balance will grow over time and reduce the equity ultimately left
to their heirs.

## Practice Quiz

1. A reverse mortgage allows a homeowner to:
   A. Take out a traditional loan with monthly payments
   B. Convert home equity into cash without selling or making monthly
      payments
   C. Avoid property tax entirely
   D. Automatically increase their home's value

2. True or False: A reverse mortgage has no effect on the equity
   available to the homeowner or their heirs later.

3. Who are reverse mortgages commonly designed for?
   A. First-time homebuyers
   B. Retirees with significant home equity seeking additional income
   C. Real estate investors flipping properties
   D. Renters without any property

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. A reverse mortgage converts equity to cash without
monthly payments.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. The loan balance grows over time, reducing future
equity.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Retirees seeking income from home equity are the
common target.

## Key Takeaways

- A reverse mortgage converts home equity into cash without monthly
  payments.
- It's commonly designed for retirees seeking additional income.
- The trade-off is reduced future equity as the loan balance grows.
