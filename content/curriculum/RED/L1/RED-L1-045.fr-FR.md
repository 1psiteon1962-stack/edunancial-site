# Prêt sur valeur domiciliaire ou marge de crédit hypothécaire : quelle différence?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define a home equity prêt and a HELOC separately.
- Explain the core structural difference between them.
- Identify a situation where each might fit better.

## Contenu principal

### Home Equity Loan

A home equity prêt provides a lump sum upfront, borrowed against the
home's equity, repaid over a fixed term with a fixed interest rate —
similar in structure to the original hypothèque itself.

### HELOC (Home Equity Line of Credit)

A HELOC works more like a credit card secured by home equity: a credit
limit is established, and the propriétaire can borrow, repay, and borrow again
up to that limit during a draw period, often with a variable interest
rate.

### When Each Tends to Fit Better

A home equity prêt often fits a one-time, known expense (like a specific
renovation with a fixed budget). A HELOC often fits ongoing or uncertain
expenses (like a renovation with an evolving scope, or an emergency
reserve), since it doesn't require borrowing the full amount upfront.

## Exemple pratique

A homepropriétaire with a clearly budgeted $30,000 kitchen renovation might
choose a home equity prêt for that fixed amount. A homepropriétaire unsure how
much a series of ongoing home projects will cost might prefer a HELOC,
borrowing only what's needed as costs arise.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. A home equity prêt typically provides:
   A. An ongoing credit line the propriétaire draws from repeatedly
   B. A lump sum upfront, repaid over a fixed term
   C. Free money with no repayment required
   D. A reduction in propriété tax

2. Vrai ou faux: A HELOC works more like a credit card secured by home
   equity, with a draw period.

3. Which is generally a better fit for a one-time, clearly known
   expense?
   A. A HELOC
   B. A home equity prêt
   C. Neither is ever appropriate
   D. Only a reverse hypothèque

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. A home equity prêt provides a lump sum, repaid over a
fixed term.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. A HELOC functions like a revolving credit line.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. A home equity prêt often fits a known, fixed expense
better.

## Points clés

- A home equity prêt provides a lump sum with fixed repayment terms.
- A HELOC provides a revolving credit line secured by home equity.
- The right choice depends on whether the expense is fixed and known or
  ongoing and uncertain.
