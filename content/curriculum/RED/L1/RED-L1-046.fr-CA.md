# Que signifie avoir une hypothèque supérieure à la valeur de la propriété?

## Objectifs d’apprentissage

À la fin de cette leçon, vous devriez pouvoir :

- Define "underwater" in a hypothèque context.
- Explain how a propriété can end up underwater.
- Explain why it creates a real problem if the propriétaire needs to sell.

## Contenu principal

### What "Underwater" Means

Being underwater on a hypothèque means the prêt balance owed is higher
than the propriété's current market valeur — in other words, negative
valeur nette, a concept introduced earlier in this track.

### How It Can Happen

This typically happens when propriété valeurs decline after purchase,
especially for propriétaires with smaller down payments (less valeur nette cushion to
begin with) or during a broader market downturn.

### Why It's a Real Problem When Selling

An underwater propriétaire who needs to sell would owe more than the vente would
generate, meaning they'd need to bring cash to clôture just to pay off
the difference — a real, stressful situation that limits an propriétaire's
options until valeurs recover or the prêt balance is paid down further.

## Exemple pratique

An propriétaire bought a home for $300,000 with a small down payment. The local
market declines, and the home is now worth $270,000, while the propriétaire
still owes $285,000 — they're underwater by $15,000.

## Questionnaire pratique

1. Being "underwater" on a hypothèque means:
   A. The home is located near water
   B. The prêt balance owed exceeds the propriété's current valeur
   C. The propriétaire has paid off the hypothèque completely
   D. The propriété has flood damage

2. Vrai ou faux: Being underwater is another way of describing negative
   valeur nette.

3. Why is being underwater a problem if the propriétaire needs to sell?
   A. It isn't a problem at all
   B. The propriétaire would owe more than the vente generates, requiring cash
      to close the gap
   C. It guarantees a faster vente
   D. It automatically cancels the hypothèque

## Corrigé

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Réponses et explications

### Question 1

Bonne réponse: B. Underwater means owing more than the propriété is
currently worth.

### Question 2

Bonne réponse: True. Underwater and negative valeur nette describe the same
situation.

### Question 3

Bonne réponse: B. Selling while underwater requires covering the gap in
cash.

## Points clés

- Being underwater means the prêt balance exceeds the propriété's valeur.
- It's the same situation as negative valeur nette.
- It creates a real financial problem if the propriétaire needs to sell.
