# What Is Short Sale, and How Is It Different from Foreclosure?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define a short sale in plain terms.
- Explain how it differs from foreclosure.
- Explain why a lender might prefer a short sale.

## Core Content

### What a Short Sale Is

A short sale happens when a struggling owner sells their property for
less than what's owed on the mortgage, with the lender's approval,
rather than going through foreclosure.

### How It Differs from Foreclosure

In a short sale, the owner (with lender approval) actively sells the
property, generally retaining more control over the process and
timeline. In foreclosure, the lender takes and sells the property through
a legal process the owner doesn't control.

### Why a Lender Might Prefer It

Foreclosure is often costly and time-consuming for lenders too, and a
foreclosed property can sell for less at auction than through a normal
sale process. A short sale can sometimes recover more value for the
lender with less cost and time than pursuing foreclosure.

## Worked Example

An owner facing financial hardship owes $280,000 on a home now worth
only $250,000. With lender approval, they sell it for $250,000 as a
short sale rather than letting the situation proceed to foreclosure.

## Practice Quiz

1. A short sale happens when:
   A. A property sells for more than the loan balance
   B. An owner sells for less than what's owed, with lender approval
   C. A lender forcibly takes the property with no owner involvement
   D. A property sells within 24 hours of listing

2. True or False: In a short sale, the owner generally retains more
   control over the process than in foreclosure.

3. Why might a lender prefer a short sale over foreclosure?
   A. Lenders never prefer short sales
   B. Foreclosure can be more costly and time-consuming, and may recover
      less value
   C. Short sales are always faster than any other option
   D. Short sales eliminate the loan entirely with no lender involvement

## Answer Key

1. B
2. True
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. A short sale is an owner-initiated sale for less than
owed, with lender approval.

### Question 2

Correct answer: True. The owner generally has more control than in a
lender-driven foreclosure.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Foreclosure's cost and time can make a short sale the
more attractive option for a lender.

## Key Takeaways

- A short sale is an owner-initiated sale for less than what's owed,
  with lender approval.
- It generally gives the owner more control than foreclosure.
- Lenders may prefer it since foreclosure can be costlier and slower.
