## Release Notes

### Version 1.0 — 2026-07-30

Initial release of RED Level 1. This level establishes the foundational knowledge required before advancing to RED Level 2.

**Coverage**: All 10 lessons cover the core disciplines of real estate investing: cash flow analysis, market evaluation, financing, property acquisition, tax lien and deed investing, creative finance strategies, property management, and portfolio construction.

**Prerequisites**: Financial Foundations (recommended) or equivalent basic financial literacy.

**Completion criteria**: Learner must achieve a passing score on the RED Level 1 knowledge check in each lesson.
