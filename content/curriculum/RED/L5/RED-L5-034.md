Here is the "why" this status generally requires a genuinely substantial, documented time commitment: REGION-SPECIFIC RULE, confirm current specific hour and participation requirements, but this status generally requires the taxpayer to spend a genuinely substantial total number of hours in real estate activities, generally representing more than half of total working hours. This is why pursuing this status involves a genuine career tradeoff for an investor substantially employed elsewhere: an investor genuinely, substantially employed in a separate career generally cannot simultaneously satisfy this hours requirement while maintaining that other career at its current full commitment level. This is why an investor should orchestrate the genuine tax benefit against these real career tradeoffs: an investor should specifically, honestly orchestrate the genuine tax savings against the genuine value of whatever career commitment would actually need to be reduced or abandoned, rather than pursuing this status purely for its tax benefit in isolation.
