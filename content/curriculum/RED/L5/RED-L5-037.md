# Property Insurance Claim Strategy After Disaster: Orchestrating Coverage Terms, Legal Rights, and Rebuild Decision

## Scenario Opener
ASSUMPTION/OPINION (hypothetical scenario for teaching purposes only): Picture a major agricultural region suffering a severe, multi-season drought that sharply cuts the global supply of a key food commodity. As a severe agricultural drought and associated wildfire risk damages a specific property, the owner discovers that a genuinely effective insurance claim strategy requires orchestrating the actual policy coverage terms, genuine legal rights within the claims process, and the eventual rebuild-versus-sell decision together.

## Learning Objectives
- Explain why a property owner should specifically, carefully review their actual policy's coverage terms following a genuine loss.
- Explain the genuine legal rights a property owner typically retains within an insurance claims process, including the right to obtain an independent damage assessment.
- Explain why the eventual rebuild-versus-sell decision should be orchestrated into the claims strategy from an early stage.
- Explain why a property owner facing a genuinely significant, complex claim should consider engaging qualified professional assistance, echoing this curriculum's broader doctor analogy.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE: A property owner facing genuine wildfire damage carefully reviews their actual policy's specific coverage terms, obtains an independent damage assessment after determining the insurer's initial offer appears inadequate, and specifically orchestrates the rebuild-versus-sell decision into their overall claims strategy from an early stage.
