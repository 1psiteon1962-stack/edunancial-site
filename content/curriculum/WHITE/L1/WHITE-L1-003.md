# What Is a Stock, Really? (You're Buying a Piece of a Company)

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define a share of stock in plain terms.
- Explain what stock ownership entitles you to, and what it does not.
- Explain why total shares outstanding matters to what one share
  represents.

## Core Content

### A Share Is a Slice of Real Ownership

When a company is public, it divides ownership into a fixed number of
shares. Owning 1,000 shares out of 1,000,000 outstanding means owning
0.1% of that company — genuinely, legally.

### What Ownership Entitles You To (and Doesn't)

Ownership typically entitles you to a proportional claim on profits (if
dividends are paid) and certain voting rights on major decisions. It
does NOT entitle you to control daily operations, a guaranteed dividend,
or a guaranteed rising price. Shareholders also generally aren't
personally liable for the company's debts beyond their shares' value.

### Why Total Share Count Matters

Owning 100 shares of a company with 1,000 shares outstanding is 10%
ownership. Owning 100 shares of a company with 10,000,000 shares
outstanding is 0.001%. The raw share count alone doesn't tell you your
actual stake.

## Worked Example

An investor owning 5,000 shares of a company with 500,000 shares
outstanding owns 5,000 ÷ 500,000 = 1% of the company.

## Practice Quiz

1. Owning a share of stock typically entitles you to:
   A. Daily control over company operations
   B. A proportional claim on profits and certain voting rights
   C. Guaranteed dividend payments
   D. Personal liability for the company's debts

2. True or False: The number of shares you own, by itself, tells you
   your ownership percentage.

3. A company has 200,000 shares outstanding. An investor owns 2,000
   shares. What percentage do they own?
   A. 0.1%
   B. 1%
   C. 2%
   D. 10%

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Ownership entitles you to proportional profit claims
and certain voting rights, not operational control.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Ownership percentage depends on shares owned
relative to total shares outstanding.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. 2,000 ÷ 200,000 = 1%.

## Key Takeaways

- A share is real, legal partial ownership in a company.
- Ownership doesn't mean operational control or a guaranteed dividend.
- Your actual ownership stake depends on shares owned relative to total
  shares outstanding.
