# What Is a Share Price, and What Moves It?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define share price in plain terms.
- Explain the basic supply-and-demand mechanism behind price changes.
- Name factors that can shift that supply and demand.

## Core Content

### What Share Price Is

Share price is the current amount a share of stock trades for on the
market — the price the most recent buyer and seller agreed to.

### The Basic Mechanism

At its core, a share price moves based on supply and demand: more buyers
than sellers at a given price tends to push the price up; more sellers
than buyers tends to push it down.

### What Shifts Supply and Demand

Company-specific news (earnings, new products, leadership changes),
broader economic conditions, industry trends, and investor sentiment can
all shift how many people want to buy or sell at a given price — which is
why prices can move even without any change to the company itself.

## Worked Example

A company reports better-than-expected profits. More investors want to
buy shares at the current price than there are sellers willing to sell,
pushing the price upward until a new balance is reached.

## Practice Quiz

1. Share price is fundamentally driven by:
   A. Government-set rules
   B. Supply and demand
   C. The company's founding date
   D. The stock's ticker symbol

2. True or False: A share price can only change if something changes
   about the company itself.

3. What is one factor that can shift supply and demand for a stock?
   A. Company earnings news
   B. The stock's ticker symbol
   C. The company's founding date
   D. The color of the company logo

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. A

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Supply and demand fundamentally drive share price.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Broader market conditions and sentiment can move
prices without any company-specific change.

### Question 3

Correct answer: A. Earnings news is a common driver of shifting
supply/demand.

## Key Takeaways

- Share price reflects the most recent agreed trade price.
- Supply and demand fundamentally drive price movement.
- Company news, economic conditions, and sentiment can all shift supply
  and demand.
