# What Is an ETF? (A Basket, Not a Single Stock)

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define an ETF in plain terms.
- Explain how it differs from buying a single stock.
- Explain one practical benefit of the basket structure.

## Core Content

### What an ETF Is

An ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund) is a fund holding a collection of assets
(often many stocks or bonds), with shares that trade on an exchange
throughout the day, similar to a single stock.

### How It Differs from a Single Stock

Buying one share of a single company's stock gives exposure to that one
company only. Buying one share of an ETF gives exposure to everything
inside that basket at once — potentially dozens or hundreds of
underlying holdings, through one purchase.

### A Practical Benefit of the Basket Structure

Because an ETF spreads exposure across many holdings at once, the
poor performance of any single holding inside it has a smaller impact on
the overall ETF than it would if that were the investor's only holding —
a basic form of diversification, covered in more depth in a later
lesson.

## Worked Example

Instead of picking one company to invest in, an investor buys one share
of an ETF that holds shares of 500 different companies — spreading their
exposure across all 500 through a single purchase.

## Practice Quiz

1. An ETF is best described as:
   A. A single company's stock
   B. A fund holding a collection of assets, tradable like a stock
   C. A type of savings account
   D. A government bond

2. True or False: Buying one share of an ETF only gives exposure to one
   underlying company.

3. What is one practical benefit of an ETF's basket structure?
   A. It guarantees profit
   B. It spreads exposure across many holdings at once
   C. It eliminates all investment risk
   D. It requires picking individual stocks

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. An ETF is a tradable fund holding multiple assets.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. An ETF share typically provides exposure to many
underlying holdings at once.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Spreading exposure across many holdings is the core
benefit.

## Key Takeaways

- An ETF is a fund holding a collection of assets, tradable like a stock.
- One ETF share can provide exposure to many underlying holdings at
  once.
- This basket structure provides a basic form of diversification.
