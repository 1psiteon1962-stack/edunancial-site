# What Is Diversification, in Plain Terms?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define diversification in plain terms.
- Explain what kind of risk it reduces.
- Explain why it doesn't eliminate all risk.

## Core Content

### What Diversification Is

Diversification means spreading investments across multiple different
assets rather than concentrating everything in one — the basic idea
behind the "basket" structure of ETFs and mutual funds covered earlier.

### What Kind of Risk It Reduces

Diversification specifically reduces concentration risk — the risk that
comes from one single holding performing badly and dragging down an
entire portfolio. If one holding among many performs poorly, its impact
on the total is smaller than if it were the only holding.

### Why It Doesn't Eliminate All Risk

Diversification doesn't protect against broader market-wide declines
that affect many or most holdings at once, and it doesn't guarantee
profit. It manages one specific type of risk, not every possible risk.

## Worked Example

An investor holding only one company's stock loses significantly if that
one company performs badly. An investor holding shares across 50
different companies is much less affected by any single company's poor
performance — though a broad market downturn could still affect all 50.

## Practice Quiz

1. Diversification means:
   A. Investing everything in one asset
   B. Spreading investments across multiple different assets
   C. Avoiding all investment risk
   D. Guaranteeing profit

2. True or False: Diversification eliminates all investment risk.

3. What specific type of risk does diversification primarily reduce?
   A. Market-wide risk
   B. Concentration risk (over-reliance on one holding)
   C. Currency risk exclusively
   D. Inflation risk exclusively

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Diversification means spreading investments across
multiple assets.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. It reduces certain risk but doesn't eliminate all
risk, especially broad market declines.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Concentration risk is the specific risk
diversification addresses.

## Key Takeaways

- Diversification means spreading investments across multiple assets.
- It specifically reduces concentration risk.
- It does not eliminate all risk or guarantee profit.
