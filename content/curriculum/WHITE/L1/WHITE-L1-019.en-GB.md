# What Is an IRA, and How Is It Different from a 401(k)?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define an IRA in plain terms.
- Explain the core difference between an IRA and a 401(k).
- Recognize that both serve the same general retirement-saving purpose.

## Core Content

### What an IRA Is

*(REGION-SPECIFIC RULE: this is a U.S.-specific account type.)* An IRA
(Individual Retirement Account) is a retirement savings account an
individual opens on their own, independent of an employer, often through
a bank or brokerage.

### The Core Difference from a 401(k)

A 401(k) is tied to an employer and typically only available while
working there. An IRA is opened independently by an individual and isn't
tied to any specific employer — someone can have an IRA whether or not
they even have a job with a 401(k) available.

### Same General Purpose

Despite the structural difference, both account types serve the same
core purpose: encouraging and providing tax-advantaged ways to save for
retirement.

## Worked Example

Someone who is self-employed, with no employer 401(k) available, can
still open and contribute to an IRA independently — the account isn't
tied to having a specific employer at all.

## Practice Quiz

1. An IRA is:
   A. Only available through an employer
   B. A retirement account an individual opens independently
   C. A type of checking account
   D. A government pension

2. True or False: A 401(k) and an IRA are tied to an employer in exactly
   the same way.

3. Can someone without an employer-sponsored 401(k) still save for
   retirement through an IRA?
   A. No, an employer is always required
   B. Yes, an IRA is opened independently of any employer
   C. Only if they are retired already
   D. Only government employees can open an IRA

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. An IRA is opened independently by an individual.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. A 401(k) is employer-tied; an IRA is not.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. IRAs are independent of employer sponsorship.

## Key Takeaways

- An IRA is a retirement account opened independently, not tied to an
  employer.
- This is the core structural difference from a 401(k).
- Both serve the same general purpose: tax-advantaged retirement saving.
