# What Is a Money Market Fund?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define a money market fund in plain terms.
- Explain what it typically invests in.
- Explain why it's generally considered lower risk than a stock fund.

## Core Content

### What a Money Market Fund Is

A money market fund is a type of mutual fund that invests in
short-term, generally high-quality debt instruments, aiming to provide
stability and liquidity rather than significant growth.

### What It Typically Invests In

Money market funds commonly hold things like short-term government
debt and other very short-term, high-credit-quality loans — investments
generally chosen for stability over growth potential.

### Why It's Generally Lower Risk

Because it holds short-term, high-quality debt, a money market fund is
generally considered lower risk and more stable in value than a stock
fund — though it's not entirely risk-free, and typically offers more
modest returns as a trade-off.

## Worked Example

An investor wanting a relatively stable place to hold cash they might
need soon, while still earning some return, might use a money market
fund rather than a stock-focused fund with more volatility.

## Practice Quiz

1. A money market fund typically invests in:
   A. High-growth technology stocks
   B. Short-term, high-quality debt instruments
   C. Real estate directly
   D. Cryptocurrency exclusively

2. True or False: Money market funds are generally considered
   higher-risk than stock funds.

3. Why might an investor use a money market fund?
   A. To maximize long-term growth potential
   B. For relative stability and liquidity
   C. To avoid all forms of risk entirely
   D. To buy real estate

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. Money market funds hold short-term, high-quality
debt.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. They're generally considered lower risk than
stock funds.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Stability and liquidity are the core appeal.

## Key Takeaways

- A money market fund invests in short-term, high-quality debt.
- It's generally considered lower risk and more stable than stock funds.
- It trades higher growth potential for stability and liquidity.
