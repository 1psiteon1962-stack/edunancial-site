# What Is an Annuity? (Introductory Version)

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define an annuity in plain terms.
- Explain the basic idea behind converting a lump sum into payments.
- Recognize that annuities vary widely in structure and complexity.

## Core Content

### What an Annuity Is

An annuity is a financial product, typically offered by an insurance
company, where a person pays a lump sum (or a series of payments) in
exchange for a stream of future payments, often used as a source of
retirement income.

### The Basic Idea

The core concept is converting an amount of money now into a defined
income stream later — providing predictability, which appeals to
people concerned about outliving their other savings.

### Real Complexity Exists

*ASSUMPTION (for teaching purposes):* Annuities come in many different
structures with varying fees, terms, and guarantees. This introductory
lesson covers only the basic concept — the real variety and complexity
of annuity products is beyond this level's scope.

## Worked Example

A retiree pays a lump sum to an insurance company in exchange for a
guaranteed monthly payment for the rest of their life — trading a large
sum now for predictable income later.

## Practice Quiz

1. An annuity generally involves:
   A. Buying a single share of stock
   B. Converting a lump sum into a stream of future payments
   C. A type of real estate purchase
   D. A government tax refund

2. True or False: All annuities have identical structures, fees, and
   terms.

3. Why might someone consider an annuity?
   A. To avoid all financial risk permanently
   B. For predictable income, often in retirement
   C. To avoid ever paying taxes
   D. To eliminate the need for any other savings

## Answer Key

1. B
2. False
3. B

## Answers and Explanations

### Question 1

Correct answer: B. An annuity converts a lump sum into future payments.

### Question 2

Correct answer: False. Annuity structures, fees, and terms vary widely.

### Question 3

Correct answer: B. Predictable income is the core appeal.

## Key Takeaways

- An annuity converts a lump sum into a stream of future payments.
- It's often used as a source of predictable retirement income.
- Annuity structures vary widely — this is only an introductory concept.
