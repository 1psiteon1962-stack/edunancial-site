## WHITE-L2-030
### Taxable vs. Tax-Advantaged: Where to Hold What

**1. Learning Objectives**
By the end of this lesson, the learner will be able to:
- Explain the general concept of "asset location" as distinct from asset allocation.
- Describe general considerations for deciding which investments to hold in which account types.
- Recognise this as an area for coordinated professional guidance.

**2. Core Content**
Building on the previous lesson, "asset location" refers to the decision of which specific investments to hold within which account types (taxable versus various tax-advantaged categories), as distinct from asset allocation (Lesson 16), which addresses the overall mix of stocks, bonds, and cash regardless of account type. An investor might hold the same overall 70/30 stock/bond allocation across multiple accounts, but thoughtfully decide which specific holdings go in which account based on their tax characteristics.

General considerations discussed in this area include placing investments that generate significant ongoing taxable income (such as certain bond interest) in tax-advantaged accounts where that income might not be currently taxed, while placing investments with more favourable existing tax treatment even in taxable accounts (such as certain long-term stock holdings, addressed further in Lesson 31) in the taxable account itself. These are general considerations discussed in personal finance education, not a one-size-fits-all formula, since actual optimal asset location depends on an individual's complete tax situation and the specific accounts available to them.

**3. Worked Example**
Working with her financial advisor, Ingrid reviews her overall 70/30 stock/bond allocation, which she holds across both a taxable brokerage account and a tax-advantaged retirement account. Rather than randomly splitting each holding evenly across both accounts, they thoughtfully place her bond holdings (which generate regular taxable interest) primarily within her tax-advantaged account, and her stock index fund holdings (which generate comparatively less current taxable activity) primarily within her taxable account — while confirming her overall 70/30 allocation remains consistent across both accounts combined.

**6. Answers and Explanations**
1. Asset location is the decision of which specific investments to hold within which account types.
2. Asset allocation addresses the overall investment mix; asset location addresses which account holds which specific investment.
3. Optimal asset location depends on an individual's complete tax situation and available account types.
4. Bond interest is often taxed as ordinary income, making tax-advantaged accounts a commonly discussed placement consideration.

**7. Key Takeaways**
- Asset location addresses which specific investments are held in which account types, distinct from asset allocation.
- General considerations include placing higher-taxable-income investments in tax-advantaged accounts where suitable.
- These are general considerations, not a universal formula, since optimal placement depends on individual circumstances.
- Coordinating asset location with a financial or tax professional helps align the overall strategy with individual tax realities.
