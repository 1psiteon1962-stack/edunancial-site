### Version 1.0 — 2026-08-03

Initial release of BLUE Level 1 — Business Foundations.

Ten lessons covering the core financial and operational competencies required for business ownership: business models, cash flow, profit margins, KPIs, pricing strategy, customer acquisition economics, operational systems, and risk management. Lesson 10 integrates all Level 1 concepts into a personal business competency assessment and 90-day action plan.
