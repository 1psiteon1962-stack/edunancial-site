## Release Notes

### Version 1.0 — 2026-08-02

Initial release of WHITE Level 1. This level establishes the foundational knowledge required for competent paper asset investing before advancing to WHITE Level 2.

**Coverage**: All 10 lessons cover the core disciplines of paper asset investing: understanding the asset class, stocks, bonds, funds, tax-advantaged accounts, asset allocation, market mechanics, financial statement analysis, risk management, and personal strategy development.

**Prerequisites**: Financial Foundations (recommended) or equivalent basic financial literacy. No prior investing experience required.

**Completion criteria**: Learner must complete all 10 lessons and produce a written personal investment strategy before advancing to WHITE Level 2.
